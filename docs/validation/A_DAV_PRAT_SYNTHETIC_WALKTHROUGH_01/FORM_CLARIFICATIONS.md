# Form clarifications from the invented pilot

These are proposed instructions for a later training-form revision. They change no frozen DAV schema, scientific rule or runtime. Form v1 and both sealed first passes remain unchanged. The clarified instructions have not received another reader pilot and are not an approved real-source form.

1. **Separate source assertions from unsupported proposals.** Both readers marked the case-2 episode question `CONFLICTING`, while explaining that the native BETA binding has explicit toy support and the legacy ALPHA proposal has none. Make that provenance difference explicit beside any conflicting value. A conflict label does not give the unsupported proposal equal authority or permit transfer.
2. **State the scope of grouped support.** The seven questions can contain supported subclaims and a missing material join. Retain both. Use `UNRESOLVED` when a material requested relation remains missing. A narrowly `SUPPORTED` proposition is not certification of the complete question domain, header linkage, branch eligibility or admission. Case 2's person/specimen/assay links are supported; its absent header and measurement contract remain absent. Later private forms should make these scope limits easy to record without collapsing grains.
3. **Permit a locator for explicit absence.** Form v1 required empty `line_ids` for `NOT_IN_INSPECTED_OBJECTS`, even though A5/B5/C4 explicitly described omitted evidence. A later revision should allow such a locator; an empty list remains appropriate when the domain is simply not addressed. Neither form permits a claim of global absence. The original v1 answers followed their locked instruction and are retained as written.
4. **Distinguish hypothetical alternatives from reader disagreement.** Case 3's X/Y interpretations are casebook alternatives, not the exercise readers' first passes or authenticated source assertions. Both readers left their selection unresolved. Preserve the alternatives without inventing an observed reader disagreement. Equal legacy bytes do not select an interpretation.

These are usability/coding clarifications, not missing custody approvals, new source evidence or established software failures. The existing procedure already blocks missing evidence and handles synthetic disagreements separately from actual authentication. Apply any future form revision under a new version/basis before its next pass; do not rewrite the old pass or reuse its seal as validation of revised instructions.
