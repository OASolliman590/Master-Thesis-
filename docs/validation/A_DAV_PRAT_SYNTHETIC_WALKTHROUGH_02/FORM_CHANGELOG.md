# Clarified training form v2

This version implements the four documentation clarifications from the [first walkthrough](../A_DAV_PRAT_SYNTHETIC_WALKTHROUGH_01/FORM_CLARIFICATIONS.md). It changes only this synthetic training form. The prior form, reader passes and seals remain unchanged. The canonical seven questions, fictional casebook and training policy are retained; no frozen schema or runtime field is extended.

| Clarification | Form-v2 change | What the rehearsal must examine |
|---|---|---|
| F1 — provenance of assertions | Typed subclaims distinguish case facts, unsupported proposals, hypothetical alternatives, unresolved gaps and scoped absence. | An unsupported legacy proposal remains distinguishable from the explicit toy binding; a conflict label grants neither equal authority nor an outcome transfer. |
| F2 — scope of grouped support | Every grouped assertion states `support_scope`, `remaining_gaps` and its component subclaims. | Supported person/specimen/assay facts do not silently certify missing exact headers, object linkage, measurement or eligibility. |
| F3 — explicit absence locators | The form allows and requests citations for explicit omission text. Empty references are reserved for a domain simply not addressed. | Case-limited absence can retain its actual toy locator; it is never global absence. |
| F4 — hypothetical alternatives | The form labels hypothetical alternatives and distinguishes them from peer-reader work. | Equal legacy bytes do not resolve the toy alternatives or manufacture an observed reader disagreement. |

The subclaim labels are training annotations, not new DAV enums, source assertions, approvals or scientific rules. The reused cases are not an unseen validation set. This is a small usability rehearsal, not a reliability study or a clinical evidence assessment.
