# Remaining steps after the training scope decision

This list separates missing authority and source evidence from software work. It describes future stages, not permission to start them. The present scope remains synthetic only. The user does not know the approval references; this list does not ask them to invent or certify those references.

| Order | Remaining action | Responsible role, not an appointed person | Completion evidence | Present state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the applicable custody/use authority and appoint two authorized readers. Confirm the public or controlled access route, terms and retention rules as applicable. | Project owner and responsible custodian/institution | Checkable authority and appointment references, with identity/authentication in the authorized system | Not established in checked records |
| 2 | After separate authorization for real-source work, inventory the exact objects and versions in approved custody. Record checksums, terms, missing objects and limits of family coverage. | Authorized custodian and readers | Object manifest and source locators tied to the review basis | Expected roles are planned; actual objects not inspected here |
| 3 | Fix the questions, coding instructions, source-supported selection policy and common basis; retain known gaps. Familiarize the actual readers with the form and lock the basis before outcome-based selection. Do not agree individual source answers in advance. | Appointed readers and accountable reviewer | Versioned basis and policy record; explicit missing evidence; training acknowledgement | Source basis and reader preparation pending |
| 4 | Obtain two separate first passes in which each reader authors identity, episode, endpoint and other source assertions independently. Seal each before comparison. Then compare, retain disagreements and authenticate review/adjudication in the authorized system. | Two appointed readers and accountable adjudicator | Independent review records, seals, retained conflicts and authenticated sign-off | Not run for real sources |
| 5 | Complete V0–V6 and independent source sign-off. Then evaluate V7 admission and obtain reviewer approval, validate V8 matrices and obtain V9 split/exposure/release approval. | Authorized review and release owners | Accepted gate records and immutable release | No source or branch admitted |

These stages follow the [two-reader plan](../../evidence/A_DAV_PRAT_SOURCE_REVIEW_PLAN_01/TWO_READER_PLAN.md) and frozen [admission/release sequence](../../../specs/A_DATA_ACQUISITION_VALIDATION/COHORT_ADMISSION_AND_RELEASE.md). They do not impose a universal IRB application on every public object; the applicable access route must be established first. No contact with a custodian or other person is authorized by this packet.

Biological analysis is a separate future task. It can start only with the required release and separate authorization. A native label alone cannot fill missing meaning, individual assessment, timing or episode evidence. Training agreements and hashes cannot supply source authority or authenticate readers.

## Software work

No software fix is required by this scope decision. Form v3 and its local rule checker are complete within the synthetic training scope. They do not replace production validators or certify the whole implementation. Any later executable failure must be recorded with a reproducible case and handled as a separate software issue. Missing approvals, missing source objects and unresolved source meaning remain authority/evidence gaps.

The source-plan E01–E08 evidence requirements still need actual source/authentication work. E09 methods choices remain deferred to the applicable stage; this form decision selects no analysis method. E10 admission remains downstream of the source review. See the [source review plan](../../evidence/A_DAV_PRAT_SOURCE_REVIEW_PLAN_01/README.md).
