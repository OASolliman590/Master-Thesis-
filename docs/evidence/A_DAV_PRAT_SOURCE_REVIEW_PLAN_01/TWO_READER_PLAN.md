# Two-reader source review plan

Scope: `Prat-GSE93157 × A_BASELINE_BIO`. This is a proposed execution order under unchanged DAV contracts. All actual stages below are **NOT_RUN**. It covers E01–E08 and P01–P08. It selects no endpoint variant, development/final-test role, numerical window, named molecular method or admission outcome.

## Roles and private working record

The source custodian maintains the exact object set and permissions. The scientific owner fixes the source-supported baseline/archival/repeat/lesion/assay policy. Two distinct authorized readers make independent first passes; their identities, qualifications for the assigned evidence and prior exposure must be verified. An authorized adjudicator resolves disputed assertions with source evidence. The integrator checks DAV consistency; a release/privacy reviewer checks any permitted export. These are proposed responsibilities, not appointed people or authenticated signatures.

Readers must not see the peer's first-pass answers or receive answer-bearing summaries before both seals. Shared source objects, questions and policy are allowed; shared authored answers are not. Record assistance, common hints and prior access truthfully. Two agents, different model names, matching hashes or a reviewer string alone do not establish independent authorized source review.

Use the fixed [REVIEW_QUESTIONS.tsv](REVIEW_QUESTIONS.tsv). In private, each granular assertion needs its question ID, raw source assertion, exact object/version and locator, interpretation/rule locator, uncertainty/conflict, author/version and evidence disposition. Group these under the seven existing question IDs without collapsing distinct patients, specimens, assays, components or assessments. Canonical IDs come from the shared DAV authority; no ID is minted from a hash prefix or guessed join.

Private working dispositions are `SUPPORTED`, `UNRESOLVED`, `CONFLICTING` or `NOT_IN_INSPECTED_OBJECTS`. They describe the examined basis, not admission or new canonical schema enums. An unavailable/uninspected object cannot support a negative source assertion. Unsupported fields stay null/unresolved.

Peer independence and outcome masking are distinct. Readers may need endpoint information to review the endpoint question after policy lock. Do not describe that work as fully outcome-blinded. If prior exposure makes outcome-masked policy setting impossible, retain a truthful exposure record and seek authorized scientific disposition; do not certify retrospective blinding or untouched-test status. General duplicate-extraction guidance does not authenticate that process. [Methods note](METHODS_NOTE.md)

The form walkthrough is also **NOT_RUN**. Proposed invented cases are an ambiguous lexical alias, a specimen/outcome episode mismatch and equal legacy table hashes with different source assertions. Use them to check that readers can record uncertainty, locators and disagreements. Clarify instructions without changing the seven question IDs or frozen scientific rules. Retain the exercise basis, observations and revisions before sealing the final common basis; do not share real-source answers. Invented-example familiarization does not certify usability on real reports or actual first-pass independence. No real-report pilot is authorized by this packet.

## Comparison and adjudication record

For each disputed assertion retain both submitted values, source/rule locators, both first-pass seals, basis/version, exact disagreement, final contract reference and the authenticated disposition. The existing procedure supports `RESOLVED_FIRST`, `RESOLVED_SECOND`, `SOURCE_BACKED_EQUIVALENT`, `UNRESOLVED` and `ADDITIONAL_OBJECTS_REQUIRED`. Treat these as compatibility vocabulary, not proof that a real review has run. Semantic equivalence needs a source-backed reason even when a table hash matches. [P03/P04](../../validation/A_DAV_PRAT_PROCEDURE_01/INDEPENDENT_PROCEDURE_REVIEW.md)

Do not infer SD from nonresponse, convert a source binary to ORR/DCR/DCB, assign individuals from publication totals or propagate an endpoint across episodes. Keep distinct source assertions and apply the existing [endpoint priority](../../../specs/A_DATA_ACQUISITION_VALIDATION/ENDPOINT_ONTOLOGY.md#conflict-resolution). A source-binary endpoint, if supported, still needs its explicit branch declaration. A lack of eligible endpoint evidence can block this source for this branch without proving that the source is globally unusable.

Object, policy, schema, question-set, code, annotation, endpoint/timing, overlap/exposure or role changes reopen their affected dependencies. Retain supersession links and original artifacts; a new basis cannot inherit a first-pass seal that covered old evidence. Scope the impact explicitly and preserve unaffected evidence only with a justified dependency record. [Rebuild triggers](../../../specs/A_DATA_ACQUISITION_VALIDATION/REPRODUCE_AND_READINESS.md#rebuild-triggers)

## Narrow local export and later work

Before any later export, freeze the exact approved narrow schema/version and validator code hash, inspect the exact artifact and reject unapproved fields or sidecars. The existing synthetic `prat-narrow-v1` boundary permits status/reason enums, approved whole-artifact hashes and fixed false actual-analysis flags. It does not prove a real exporter or source system safe. The existing software's `classification` remains `SYNTHETIC_ONLY`; do not relabel it to manufacture a real-source receipt. A real operational export needs a separately reviewed route that preserves the same or narrower permission. [Existing public summary](../../../tools/a_dav/prat_review.py)

No patient/canonical IDs, sample headers, dates, raw values, record/class counts, class-presence indicators, reader identities, source locators, private paths, free text, per-patient hashes or alternate attachments may be exported into this local evidence packet. Whole-artifact digests may identify approved retained reviews; they must not become a patient linkage channel. The generic DAV aggregate policy does not broaden this source-specific boundary. The documentation and requirement IDs here are public planning content, not an export of real review results.

Only after actual evidence and review have been accepted in approved custody may an integrator separately propose a real-source conversion/admission scope. Existing synthetic-only tools must not be switched to real input as a shortcut. The frozen sequence remains evidence V0–V6, independent source sign-off, V7 admission/reviewer approval, then separately authorized V8/V9 work. No branch is released by completing this plan.
