# GSE176307 sources and acquisition notes

## Cohort fit

GSE176307 is a public RNA-seq cohort of advanced/metastatic urothelial carcinoma patients treated with immune checkpoint blockade; GEO describes 103 treated patients in the study summary and lists 90 RNA-seq samples. The publication reports sequencing from 89 patients, and GEO explicitly notes that BACI165 was sequenced twice. Thus the acquisition inventory is **90 sample records / 89 title-derived patient codes**, with one repeated patient profile to retain and flag. [GEO series record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=self&form=text&view=brief); [GEO sample records](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief); [primary article, Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8548561/)

The data are suitable for `A_BASELINE_BIO` at the source-availability level: a literature review classifies the 89-patient GSE176307 cohort as pretreatment, and the original paper says archived FFPE tumour tissue from primary or metastatic sites was used. GEO itself does not supply an explicit pre/on-treatment sample characteristic or individual biopsy date, so the field map records no sample-level visit field and does not infer collection dates. [Systematic review cohort table](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9153191/); [primary article, Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8548561/); [GEO sample records](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief)

GEO `!Sample_title` is `Patient sample BACI<number>` (with `_1` and `_2` on the two BACI165 records), which supports a title-based patient-code candidate. GEO has no timepoint characteristic. `io.response` is the per-sample response label; `io.therapy` is the drug; `io.lot` is the ICI line; `primary tumor location` describes the primary cancer site, not necessarily the sampled lesion. GEO's source description says metastatic UC, while the paper says the sequenced material is archived FFPE tumour from primary or metastatic site. [GEO sample records](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief); [primary article, Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8548561/)

The per-patient source of record for best response is the raw GEO `io.response` characteristic because it is the public source with a per-sample response value. The observed vocabulary is CR, PR, SD, PD, and NA; one sample is NA, and the two BACI165 records both carry PD. The paper reports response outcomes and objective response in aggregate and describes its cohort response analysis, but no author-provided per-patient BOR table was located in the linked article or GEO record. Preserve GEO raw labels; map CR/PR as objective responders, SD/PD as nonresponders, and NA as unknown only when applying the declared endpoint ontology. [GEO sample records](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief); [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8548561/); [GEO series record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=self&form=text&view=brief)

The cohort is a retrospective real-world single-centre ICI cohort, not an identified prospective trial. GEO sample characteristics list pembrolizumab, atezolizumab, nivolumab, durvalumab, and avelumab; the paper describes anti-PD-1 or anti-PD-L1 treatment. GEO `io.lot` gives the immunotherapy line, but the inspected sources do not establish per-patient prior immunotherapy exposure; this remains UNKNOWN. [GEO sample records](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief); [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8548561/)

## Expression representation

Use `GSE176307_BACI_log_trans_normalized_RNAseq.csv.gz`. GEO's processing text says reads were aligned to GRCh38 v.22, quantified with RSEM against the GRCh38 transcriptome, filtered to 16,901 protein-coding genes using Ensembl annotation, and transformed as `log2(1+x)`, median-centered, and upper-quartile-scaled for other analyses. GEO describes the chosen supplementary object as log-transformed and filtered read-count output. The exact post-scaling unit and gene row identifier type are not established by the GEO processing text, so the field map records the feature identifier system as UNKNOWN and does not infer it from the Ensembl annotation statement. [GEO sample records and data_processing fields](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE176307&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief); [GEO supplementary listing](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE176nnn/GSE176307/suppl/)

GEO's supplementary directory reports the selected expression object as 6.6 Mb and the family SOFT object as 13 K. These reported rounded listing sizes and their URLs are recorded in the fetch plan; together they are far below the 500 MB cohort cap. No matrix or other object over 1 MB was downloaded. The GEO rnaseq_counts path for this series returns 404, so no NCBI-generated counts object is included. [GEO supplementary listing](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE176nnn/GSE176307/suppl/); [GEO SOFT listing](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE176nnn/GSE176307/soft/); [GEO rnaseq_counts directory](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/rnaseq_counts/GSE176nnn/GSE176307/)
