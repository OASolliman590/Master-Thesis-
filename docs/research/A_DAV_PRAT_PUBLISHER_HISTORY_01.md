# Prat Table S1 publisher/platform history record

**Checked:** 24 October 2026. **Scope:** first-party AACR and Silverchair records about the publisher platform transition relevant to the 2017 Prat et al. *Cancer Research* article (DOI [10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-16-3556](https://doi.org/10.1158/0008-5472.CAN-16-3556)), plus the current AACR supplement-publication policy. This is documentary provenance research only. No spreadsheet contents were opened or analyzed. No frozen specification or biological decision is changed.

AACR's current editorial-policy page says that AACR supplementary data items are automatically published in Figshare upon publication of the final article version ([AACR editorial policies](https://aacrjournals.org/pages/editorial-policies)). This policy documents the present general workflow. By itself it does not establish that the same workflow applied to this 2017 item, that the 2023 item resulted from the 2022 platform migration, or that the deposited file is byte-identical to an original 2017 attachment.

## Item-specific provenance limit

The first-party records above support a general publisher/platform history: AACR announced a Silverchair migration in 2021, completed the journal-site transition in February 2022, and has a current Figshare record for this supplement dated March 2023. The records retrieved for this lane do not connect those events with an item-specific migration receipt, replacement notice, correction history, or explanation for the Figshare item's posting date. They provide no historical 2017 attachment checksum or comparison against independently located 2017 bytes. Accordingly, the platform transition is a documented contextual event, not proof that this Table S1 file was migrated, republished, replaced, corrected, or preserved byte-for-byte.

This was a bounded review of the cited AACR/Silverchair announcements, the AACR migration FAQ and the current AACR policy page, alongside the current item record already recorded in the companion history note. No item-specific migration statement or attachment-continuity evidence was located in those records. That bounded result does not establish global absence of internal publisher records, inaccessible or withdrawn items, or archive snapshots. It does not resolve the 2017 file-level locator or original-to-current byte identity.
