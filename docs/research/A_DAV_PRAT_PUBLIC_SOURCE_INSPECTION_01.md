## Publication-level methods: candidate rules, not individual evidence

AACR's **Patients and Methods** (subsections “Patient data,” “Sample data,” “Gene expression analysis,” and “Statistical analysis”; [version-of-record article](https://aacrjournals.org/cancerres/article-split/77/13/3540/619915/Immune-Related-Gene-Expression-Profiling-After-PD)) reports that response was evaluated 6–8 weeks after treatment began using modified RECIST 1.1; OR denotes CR/PR, while NPD includes CR/PR or SD lasting at least 3 months. Tumors were obtained before anti-PD1 treatment; the article distinguishes baseline samples collected within 3 months before treatment without intervening therapy from archival samples collected more than 3 months earlier. Its assay description reports FFPE tissue, nCounter profiling, log2 transformation, and normalization using selected housekeeping genes with nSolver 2.6; raw expression data are associated with GSE93157.

These are study-level descriptions, not proof of an individual response assessment, specimen date, same-episode linkage, or assay-file interpretation. They may be presented to the accountable reviewers as source-backed policy candidates; they do not select a DAV endpoint, settle any unresolved boundary/episode rule, or change the frozen contract. Any common-basis choice and approval remain pending. No individual rows or values were inspected here.

## Public access and use limits

NCBI's [GEO FAQ](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/faq.html) says anyone can download public GEO data without login. Its [disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html) says NCBI imposes no restrictions while noting possible submitter intellectual-property claims and that NCBI does not verify biological validity. These support public access, not a project-specific custody, permitted processing/export, or sign-off determination. The AACR Figshare Table S1 item carries its own CC BY 4.0 license; do not extend that item license to the article or unrelated GEO materials.
