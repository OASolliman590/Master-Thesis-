# Prat public-record gap assessment: GSE93157

**Checked:** 24 October 2026. **Scope:** bounded inspection of official GEO records, the publisher's version-of-record article and an indexed Methods search excerpt, publisher supplement metadata, PubMed citation/correction discovery, and a limited search for published reuse of GSE93157. This note answers what public records can establish at the study/object level. It is not a source inventory, human review, admission, or release. No individual clinical rows, sample identifiers, molecular values, alias joins, or canonical mappings were parsed or exported. Frozen specifications and prior evidence packets were not changed.

## Public records and bounded search

NCBI GEO identifies GSE93157 as a public series, cites PMID 28487385, and lists platform GPL19965. Its series page describes processed data as included in sample tables and lists a supplementary raw-values object. GPL19965 names the nCounter PanCancer Immune Profiling Panel. The public platform annotation exposes fields such as official symbol, accession, alias, target class, sequence, full name, and spot ID. These are platform-level labels; they do not establish exact study-file headers, a study-sample-to-specimen link, normalization scale, or feature coverage for a selected object. [GSE93157](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE93157), [GPL19965](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GPL19965)

## Completion boundary

Public inspection has narrowed the repository questions: relevant GEO/platform records are discoverable, the public Figshare version list currently contains one version, and public dataset reuse exists. The parent reproduced the indexed primary biopsy-timing and assay-processing passages; exact response-method wording was not independently reproduced in this recheck. Full-text capture and approved source-policy decisions remain pending. These leads do not complete E01–E08 because the unresolved items include individual relational evidence, exact-object measurement provenance, custody/terms decisions, project exposure history, and authenticated human review.

No new software defect is demonstrated. Existing disposition routes remain appropriate; missing evidence, custody, reviewer authority, or source-policy decisions are not software fixes. No biological analysis, statistics, normalization, gene computation, admission, or release was performed or authorized by this note. The frozen specification remains unchanged. The public-record closure includes the current Figshare version-list result documented in the completed [supplement history assessment](A_DAV_PRAT_TABLE_S1_HISTORY_01.md); original 2017 attachment continuity, retained full-text capture and independent response-passage verification remain open, and no public-only action can close the individual review and authority requirements.
