## C: measured corroboration has real records and unresolved joins

GSE199800 is the originating OncoLoop study, already a close novelty precedent. It contains12 candidate records for decitabine, entinostat and EPZ-6438/tazemetostat across DU145 and LNCaP. Each gene-count header contains the eight proposed Entrez IDs; neither complete count matrix nor all candidate rows were verified. The source matrices are samples-by-genes, semicolon-delimited. Distinct plate/GSM records do not prove independent culture/day replication. [OncoLoop](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-22-0342), [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE199800).

The actual publisher methods specify six plate-matched DMSO controls. Deposited `UNTREATED` labels cannot yet be mapped conclusively to the separately described water controls. SupplementS5A's `HTS_cmax (uM)` is clinical exposure metadata, not the assay-dose column; its values differ from GEO title doses, and EPZ-6438 lacks that Cmax entry. Thus the GEO numerical assay-dose units remain unresolved. Raw count `sample_id` values omit line/plate/well prefixes; the12 candidate GSM titles collapse to6 duplicated drug/dose/time suffix keys. A plate/replicate/control join is not established by stripping those prefixes.

PM154/GSE216053 has six measured expression columns and all eight proposed gene IDs finite, but treated titles describe decitabine while their genotype fields say DNMT1 knockout. The series also describes three conditions although six samples form two title groups. Preserve the contradiction and withhold pure drug-validation claims. The actual IFNG payload route returned503; a known ligand label is not usable prostate perturbation evidence. [PM154 source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE216053), [associated study](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.adf6732), [documented ligand service](https://maayanlab.cloud/L1000CDS2/help/).
