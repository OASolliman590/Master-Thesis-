# GSE248014 source review


## R/NR definition and places searched

The ClinicalTrials.gov study results define the primary objective response rate as patients achieving “a complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) based on RECIST 1.1 criteria”; the same outcome description states that patients who discontinue for clinical progression before post-baseline tumour assessment are non-responders. This appears under **Study Results > Outcome Measures > Objective Response Rate (ORR) Using RECIST 1.1**. No duration threshold for R/NR is stated there. [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03250273, Study Results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03250273?rank=6&tab=results&term=entinostat&viewType=Table)

The article’s **Figure 2 legend** identifies panel b as efficacy by best overall response by RECIST 1.1, and its **Figure 5 legend** expands “R” and “NR” as responders and non-responders. The article also reports three partial responses. GEO’s `recist` characteristic assigns the binary labels per patient: the 45 samples represent 25 patients; among 23 Baseline samples, there are 23 unique patient IDs, and three unique patients are labeled Responder. The unique-patient count agrees with the article’s three responders; do not interpret five repeated sample labels as five responding patients. [PubMed article and figure legends](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39532835/) · [GEO sample metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE248014&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief)

I searched the original article’s methods, response assessment and figure legends; the linked trial results and protocol; GEO series and sample descriptions; the GEO supplement listings; and GitHub repository search for GSE248014. The paper states RECIST v1.1 and the trial registry supplies the explicit CR/PR binary ORR rule; no clinical-benefit duration threshold or irRECIST rule was found for this cohort. GEO gives the label values but does not itself define their clinical criteria. The article’s code-availability statement points to Zenodo, not an author GitHub repository. [Article, Methods/Figure 2/Figure 5/Code availability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557583/) · [ClinicalTrials.gov results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03250273?rank=6&tab=results&term=entinostat&viewType=Table) · [GEO GSE248014 record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE248014) · [GitHub repository search API](https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=GSE248014)

## Field evidence and claims

- **Study and treatment:** GEO describes a phase II PDAC cohort treated with entinostat and nivolumab. Patients received a 14-day entinostat-only lead-in, then combination entinostat plus nivolumab. The linked article identifies trial NCT03250273 and the combination schedule. [GEO series record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE248014&targ=self&form=text&view=brief) · [Baretti et al., Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557583/) · [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03250273](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03250273)
- **Prior immunotherapy:** the public protocol eligibility criteria exclude prior immune-checkpoint inhibitor treatment. That is a trial-level rule; public GEO metadata do not provide patient-level eligibility confirmation. [NCT03250273 protocol, eligibility criteria](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/73/NCT03250273/Prot_SAP_000.pdf) · [NCI trial record](https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials-search/v?id=NCI-2019-00401)
- **Patient identity:** the GEO title pattern is `patient <ID> at time point <visit>`; the corresponding characteristics explicitly use `patient id`. The field map uses the characteristic rather than deriving patient identity from the title. [GEO sample metadata, <GSM sample>](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=<GSM sample>)
- **Timepoint:** the characteristic key is `time point`, with values including `Baseline`, `C1D1`, and `C2D1`. The paper defines Baseline biopsy before study treatment, C1D1 after the two-week entinostat lead-in, and C2D1 after six weeks of combination therapy; only Baseline is selected as pretreatment. [GEO sample metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=<GSM sample>) · [Baretti et al., Correlative science](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557583/)
- **Response:** GEO uses `recist` with values `Responder` and `NonResponder`; the same label appears across multiple timepoint samples from a patient. The 45 samples cover 25 patients; 23 samples are Baseline from 23 patients, and three unique patients have the Responder label. The article reports three partial responses. This supports use of the binary R/NR source label, but not per-patient CR/PR-level BOR. [GEO sample metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=<GSM sample>) · [GEO brief sample metadata endpoint](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE248014&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief) · [Baretti et al., Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557583/) · [Baretti et al., Data availability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557583/)

## Open questions

- Public patient-level CR/PR-level BOR detail is unavailable; the dbGaP clinical data are controlled access. GEO’s source binary R/NR label is available and its definition is specified in the trial results, but the individual BOR category (CR, PR, SD or PD) cannot be established from public data. [Baretti et al., Data availability](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557583/) · [dbGaP study record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs003615.v1.p1)
- The paper reports three partial responses, while GEO labels three unique patients as Responders. No conflict in the binary responder count is apparent; exact correspondence of individual GEO labels to article patients is not publicly established. Do not infer patient identities across differently coded sources. [GEO brief sample metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE248014&targ=gsm&form=text&view=brief) · [Baretti et al., Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557583/)
- GEO processing text does not establish the gene identifier system, annotation release, or whether the RSEM estimates are gene-level or transcript-level in the delivered table. The stated `RSEM expected counts` and hg38 assembly are the only representation attributes asserted here. [GEO sample processing metadata](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=<GSM sample>)
- Trial eligibility excludes prior checkpoint inhibitor use, but participant-specific compliance is unavailable in public sample metadata. [NCT03250273 protocol](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/73/NCT03250273/Prot_SAP_000.pdf) · [GEO GSE248014 record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE248014)
