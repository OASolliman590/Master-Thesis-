## Source findings and field rules

- **Patient identity:** GEO sample `title` values contain patient tokens and `description` identifies “MGH Patient …”. Matching the title-token capture to the description across all 10 PRE records gives 10 distinct patient descriptions: one patient per baseline sample. The field map uses the title token as the patient identifier rule. [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz)
- **Timepoint:** the characteristic `treatment state` supplies `PRE` or `ON`; the separate `timepoint (days)` characteristic supplies a day value. PRE is used to select baseline specimens. The paper says the cohort has both pretreatment and on-treatment biopsies. [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz); [paper methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- **Paper search coverage:** the Methods section “Melanoma datasets and patient selection” gives the category rule. Figure 1 legend expands R and NR but does not add criteria; the supplementary materials and linked supplement listing were also checked. No supplement is needed to recover the rule in Methods. Supplementary data files were not downloaded because they may contain patient-level material and some listed files exceed the 1 MB limit. [Gao et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- **Response source and definition:** GEO’s `response immune checkpoint blockade therapy` characteristic is the source of record for each deposited raw patient label (`R`/`NR`); the paper is the source of record for their definition. In the Methods section “Melanoma datasets and patient selection,” the paper gives the R wording “CR, PR, or stable disease (SD) with progression-free survival (PFS) longer than 180 days” and the NR wording “progressive disease (PD) or SD with PFS shorter than 180 days.” It states that response was assessed using RECIST criteria, without naming a RECIST version. Thus R = CR/PR or SD with PFS >180 days; NR = PD or SD with PFS <180 days. The wording leaves SD with PFS exactly 180 days unclassified. The paper does not specify whether its RECIST implementation is version 1.0 or 1.1. [Gao et al., 2021, Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/); [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz)
- **Tissue:** GEO `source_name_ch1` says “frozen or formalin-fixed tumor”; `tumor type` says metastatic melanoma. This supports the `TUMOUR` compartment. [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz)
- **Treatment:** sample characteristics name `anti-PD1` or `anti-PDL1`. The paper describes the cohort as metastatic melanoma patients treated with anti-PD1 therapy and discusses anti-PD1-based datasets. The GEO sample-level antibody field is retained because it distinguishes the deposited samples. A named clinical trial or one uniform drug regimen is not established by the reviewed sources. [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz); [paper methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- **Prior immunotherapy:** UNKNOWN. The reviewed GEO characteristics and paper methods do not establish each patient's prior immunotherapy exposure or a cohort-wide treatment-naive status. [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz); [paper methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- **Expression representation:** the selected supplementary object is a featureCounts read-count matrix, so the unit is read counts. GEO processing text gives `featureCounts v1.5.1` and `Genome_build: hg19`. The article's methods state that qualified reads were counted and describe generating RPKM for downstream analyses; that does not change the selected GEO object from the separately described count matrix into RPKM. The selected object’s transform and exact feature identifier/annotation type are UNKNOWN from the allowed processing text; no conversion is authorized. [GEO series processing and supplementary listing](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE168204); [paper methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- **Expected samples:** GEO lists 27 samples total. Counting the family metadata's `treatment state` values gives 10 PRE specimens across 10 described patients. This count is a source-metadata count, not a QC-passed or analysis denominator. [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE168204); [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz)
- **Directory cross-checks:** the GEO matrix listing contains two small platform-specific series-matrix metadata files (2.3 KB and 2.7 KB); they were not selected because the family SOFT provides the sample annotations and the supplementary count matrix is the needed expression object. The rnaseq_counts directory was checked; no GSE168204-generated counts object was selected. [GEO matrix directory](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/matrix/); [NCBI rnaseq_counts directory](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/rnaseq_counts/)

## Open questions

- Which RECIST version was applied? The paper says only “RECIST criteria.” [Gao et al., 2021, Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- How should SD with PFS exactly 180 days be classified? The published binary rule uses strictly longer and strictly shorter than 180 days, without assigning equality. [Gao et al., 2021, Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- What are the specific drugs represented by the `anti-PD1` and `anti-PDL1` labels, and the clinical trial or treatment protocol for each patient? [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz); [paper methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- Did any patient receive prior immunotherapy? [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz); [paper methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8519947/)
- What transform and exact gene identifier/annotation system were used for the selected count matrix? GEO states the featureCounts version and hg19 build but not these details in the processing text. [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE168204)
- Is each title token a stable patient identifier across every sample, particularly titles with nonstandard suffixes? The GEO descriptions corroborate patient labels, but those labels should be verified at ingestion before patient joins are accepted. [GEO family SOFT](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE168nnn/GSE168204/soft/GSE168204_family.soft.gz)
