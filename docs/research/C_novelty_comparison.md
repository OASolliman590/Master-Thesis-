# Paper C: closest-study comparison and proposed contribution

Evidence date: 2026-09-05. This is a bounded bibliographic assessment, not a systematic review, novelty guarantee, implementation plan, or experimental protocol. It preserves the agreed Paper C source architecture: patient expression/methylation, LINCS, CCLE/DepMap, pharmacology resources, Perturb-CITE-seq, TISMO and the proposed interferon reference. No matrices were downloaded.

## Assessment

The proposed paper cannot claim novelty merely from combining prostate transcriptomics, drug-signature reversal, immune annotations, sensitivity information and drug-target databases. Close precedents already cover those elements. The defensible candidate contribution is more specific: **determine whether patient-supported methylation information and separately assessed immune-context evidence materially change the interpretation of prostate epigenetic-drug reversal compared with expression-only prioritisation.** That contribution is proposed, not demonstrated. It requires substantive results beyond recovering known HDAC/EZH2 immune associations.

Six works were selected because they overlap the actual question, computational strategy, disease context or mechanistic claim. Five include substantial transcriptomic/computational analysis; Morel is included chiefly as the mechanistic precedent that bounds our claims. Bibliographic identifiers and findings below were checked against primary journal pages, original papers in PMC, or PubMed records.

## What the restored resources can add—and what they cannot establish by their presence

The following are **reviewer-facing interpretations and proposed contribution criteria**, not claims that analyses have been completed.

| Proposed addition | A scientifically distinct answer would be… | An insufficient novelty claim would be… |
|---|---|---|
| Patient methylation information | Whether methylation-associated immune deficits explain candidate-specific reversal evidence beyond expression-only disease signatures. | “We added TCGA methylation.” |
| LINCS plus measured prostate evidence | Whether inferred reversal persists across independent available prostate expression evidence, including meaningful discrepancies. | “Several reversal algorithms identified the same familiar drugs.” |
| CCLE/DepMap and sensitivity context | Whether the interpretation changes when molecular context and general fitness effects are separated from the immune-program question. | “The targets are expressed and essential, therefore they regulate the immune genes.” |
| DrugBank/ChEMBL and pharmacology | Whether documented pharmacology changes the confidence or scope of the computational conclusion, with missing evidence visible. | “The compounds have database entries, so they are clinically feasible.” |
| Perturb-CITE-seq and TISMO | Whether independently sourced functional or immune-context observations support, contradict or fail to cover specific parts of the inferred mechanism. | “More databases agree, so prostate ICI sensitisation is validated.” |
| Interferon reference | Whether a response is specifically consistent with the intended reference, rather than assuming every immune-associated expression change is equivalent. | “The reference contains interferon, so reversal proves immune conversion.” |

These distinctions follow from the overlap documented in the six primary studies. They require source independence and endpoint alignment; the number of resources is not itself evidence of either. In particular, Chang already combines several reversal/sensitivity resources, MD-Miner already integrates target information, OncoLoop already addresses immune-related consequences of prostate drug inversion, and Xiong already combines bulk/single-cell immune context. See the directly linked original studies above.

## Grilling questions for the scientific charter

| Question | Evidence-based answer today | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Is the broad integration concept new? | Closely related combinations are already published, especially Chang 2022, MD-Miner and OncoLoop. | Established precedent. |
| Is immune restoration by HDAC/EZH2 inhibition in prostate cancer a new central finding? | The cited HDAC transcriptomic and EZH2 studies precede it. | Established precedent. |
| Is the exact full resource combination absent from the literature? | This bounded search cannot establish absence, and absence of the exact combination would not alone establish scientific novelty. | Unknown; do not claim priority. |
| What is the strongest candidate addition? | A patient-methylation-supported, comparative account of immune-program reversal that demonstrates what orthogonal context changes relative to an expression-only account. | Proposed research contribution, not observed result. |
| Must the result be a new compound? | No. A well-supported difference in contextual validity or failure of a commonly assumed interpretation could be informative; merely rediscovering familiar compounds is weak. | Scientific judgement, not publication guarantee. |
| Does the supplied data architecture already prove that addition feasible? | No. Accession-level coverage, independent comparisons and compatible endpoints still need verification. | Open evidence requirement. |

## Search scope and limits

Queries combined prostate cancer, epigenetic/HDAC/EZH2, immune/methylation, LINCS/CMap, drug repurposing, and network/sensitivity terms. Primary pages and original-paper records were preferred. Search results included a 2026 prostate immune-cell transcriptome-wide Mendelian-randomisation preprint ([medRxiv, DOI 10.64898/2026.03.10.26348013](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.10.26348013v1.full)); it concerns a different inferential question and was not treated as one of the six closest comparators. Its preprint status does not establish peer review. No assertion is made that this search exhausted relevant preprints or publications.

Some PMC page opens encountered browser checks; corresponding primary publisher pages or PubMed records supplied the verified bibliographic details. The original-paper source support is identified in each row. None of the cited reported analyses was independently reproduced here. No current source release was inferred from historical sample counts.

## Recommendation for the next specification revision

**Retain Paper C and its full restored source architecture, but revise the novelty claim before calling the spec publication-ready.** State the comparative question above and explain the result that each source can change. If the ultimate manuscript only produces an annotated list that recovers known HDAC/EZH2 immune biology, its distinct contribution remains weak; it may fit better as the pharmacological interpretation of Paper B. A standalone decision should follow the data audit and substantive evidence, not the length of the source list.
