# Verification of AI-generated resource leads (2026-11-01)

**What was checked:** each lead was checked against its paper (PubMed / PMC), its official site (one polite request, with retries spaced out), its public API or GitHub repository, and the ClinicalTrials.gov / EGA records it cites.

**What was not done:**
- No data matrices were downloaded.
- No site protection was bypassed.
- Nothing was installed or committed.
- No patient-level detail was recorded.
- Loading one package's `.rda` index file in R was refused by the session's permission rules, so that index was not read.

**Comparison sets:** `docs/cohorts/REGISTRY.tsv` (45 rows), `docs/integration/20261030-data-assessment/ICBATLAS_INTAKE.md` and `docs/research/ICB_DATABASES_HARVEST_20261030.md`.

**Verdict rule:** the four-test rule in `specs/amendments/2026-10-30-source-strategy/DECISION_PENDING.md`. All four must hold:
1. a licence permits use;
2. the processing is documented well enough to pass V3;
3. patient IDs trace back to the original study;
4. it provides a cohort not obtainable from the original authors, GEO or cBioPortal.

Otherwise the verdict is CROSS_CHECK_ONLY, or NOT_USABLE when there is no usable data route. DOES_NOT_EXIST applies when the named resource could not be found.

**Datasets (API, study level):** Braun 2020; Hugo 2016; Nathanson 2017; Jung 2019; **Kim 2018 (45, "RECIST v1.1", 12 responders)**; Liu 2019; Mariathasan 2018; MGH 2021 (= GSE168204, PMID 34654806); Miao 2018; **Motzer 2020 (JAVELIN Renal 101)**; Snyder 2017; Van Allen 2015; Gide 2019; **Lee 2020**; Riaz 2017; plus the sub-splits.

**New cohorts beyond our three files:**
- **Lee 2020** (*Nat Commun*, PMID 32312968, "Transcriptional downregulation of MHC class I and melanoma de-differentiation in resistance to PD-1 inhibition").
  - Melanoma, anti-PD-1: "94 longitudinal melanoma biopsies" from "68 patients… 53 tumor biopsies were pre-treatment (PRE) and 41 were taken while on-treatment". ICB-Portal lists 44 pre-treatment, irRECIST.
  - Raw data: "deposited in the European Genome-phenome Archive (EGA) under study accession number EGAS00001001552, and dataset accession EGAD00001005738" (**controlled**). A processed copy is in ICB-Portal.
  - **Possible patient overlap with Gide 2019** (same Melanoma Institute Australia group): flag, do not certify independence.
- **JAVELIN Renal 101** (Motzer 2020, *Nat Med*, PMID 32895571, PMC8493486).
  - **COMBINATION** (avelumab + axitinib vs sunitinib).
  - Data availability: "Clinical response data are in Supplementary Table 11… Normalized RNA-seq data are in Supplementary Table 13". This is an **open supplement route**. ICB-Portal lists 354 samples, labelled "PFS > 6 months".

**Kim 2018 response:** ICB-Portal is the first resource found that labels Kim per patient as "RECIST v1.1" (12 responders of 45). It is still a secondary, relabelled source. Its provenance for Kim's labels is not stated (UNVERIFIED).

**Verdict: CROSS_CHECK_ONLY.** It fails tests 1 (no data licence) and 2 (no uniform or documented processing; author-supplied matrices), and it ships precomputed scores. It is useful as a pointer to Lee 2020 and JAVELIN Renal 101, and as a cross-check for Kim's response labels.
