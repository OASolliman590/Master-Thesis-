## Evidence anchors and boundaries

- [Ayers et al.](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/91190): response-associated expression programs span multiple immune functions; association in treated cohorts is not treatment benefit versus a non-ICI comparator.
- [Guo et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37327786/): prostate immune-gene repression can involve hypomethylated chromatin domains; promoter hypermethylation is not a universal mechanism.
- [MethylCIBERSORT original study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-05570-1): methylation-based composition estimation, with reference/assay limits.
- [EZH2 prostate perturbation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33899001/): mechanistic precedent means generic epigenetic priming is not itself novel.

Novelty remains conditional on exact comparison to existing studies and actual results. Previously seen metadata, expression coverage and published findings are retained in the exposure ledger. TCGA/CPC localized disease cannot alone establish applicability to treated metastatic disease.
