## Formal handling

For each deviation retain old text, scientific rationale, affected estimand/population, date, user decision, supervisor/institutional determination, exposure state and before/after contract hashes. Do not overwrite the submitted protocol or describe these proposals as institutionally approved. Ask the supervisor which changes require a formal amendment and record the answer; no legal or institutional rule is assumed here.

The detection-P correction is methodological, while changing a clinical reference, candidate-selection axis or endpoint can change the scientific question. Keep that distinction explicit. Balanced sample sizes, raw reprocessing, optional assays and new model systems are not automatically required simply because an older plan mentions them.

The protocol's actual seven clinical accessions are GSE91061, GSE78220, GSE176307, GSE135222, GSE115821, SRP094781 and SRP183455. This list records submitted scope, not seven admitted independent cohorts. Treatment timing, response definition and original-study overlap belong to A's admission ledger.
