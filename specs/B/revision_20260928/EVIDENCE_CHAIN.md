# What each arrow can establish

PROPOSED. These are claim boundaries, not completed findings.

| Arrow | Evidence status under proposed design | What is directly measured | Remaining assumption or test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observed ICI outcome → A programme association | Association, if qualifying A analysis is completed | Patient outcome and sampled expression | Treated-cohort association is not ICI treatment benefit versus no ICI; timing and heterogeneity matter. |
| A programme → prostate alteration | Cross-context inference supported by B expression comparisons | Expression in the declared prostate/reference samples | Same programme measurement must be defensible; tissue lineage and cellular mixture can explain differences. |
| Prostate alteration → epigenetic explanation | Conditional association plus triangulation | Linked RNA/methylation; measured alternatives on actual subsets | Residual confounding, reverse direction and mixture remain possible. |
| Epigenetic association → possible reversibility | Hypothesis; relevant prior perturbation may corroborate | B alone does not measure reversibility | Context-matched perturbation is required; copy-number/sequence constraints may defeat restoration. |
| Candidate → intervention nomination | Prespecified evidence-based prioritisation | In C, only actual measured perturbation supports a reversal claim | Model, dose, exposure, pharmacology and bidirectional query validity must be qualified. |
| Molecular restoration → immune function/ICI sensitisation | Experimentally testable later; unsupported as a new B result | Appropriate future molecular and functional readouts | Re-expression, presentation, T-cell function and ICI interaction are distinct steps; clinical benefit requires further clinical evidence. |

Negative, missing and contradictory evidence may break any arrow. Do not label the entire chain validated because one prediction model has positive external Delta_R2.
