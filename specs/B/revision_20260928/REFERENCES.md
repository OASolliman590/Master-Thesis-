## Newer conditional leads, not admitted validation

Song et al. 2026 integrated single-cell preprint, DOI [10.64898/2026.03.25.711335](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.25.711335); Martinelli et al. 2026 spatial-protein preprint, DOI [10.64898/2026.04.30.721907](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.30.721907). Treat as preprints with unresolved source overlap/processed-object coverage, not additional guaranteed independent patients.

Xu et al. 2025 m6A prostate multi-omics, DOI [10.1038/s41588-025-02128-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02128-y), is an optional RNA-modification question with ICGC-PRAD-CA reuse. RNA m6A is not DNA methylation. It is not required for the current B question.

PCaDB original-cohort discovery, CELLxGENE catalogue leads and PDC/PRIDE searches remain source-discovery routes with incomplete qualification. A missing qualified hit is not evidence that the repository has no prostate data.
