## B-P measurement audit

Current proposed P is `HLA-A HLA-B HLA-C B2M TAP1 TAP2 PSMB8 PSMB9`. It covers selected MHC-I-related components, not the complete antigen-presentation machinery, MHC-II, or a validated ICI assay. The biological rationale is pathway coverage; its exact equal-weight composite still requires approval. Published membership/function evidence does not validate the constructed score [R25].

For fixed common universe U, `q_i(g)=(midrank_i(g)-1)/(|U|-1)` and `Y_i=mean_{g in P} q_i(g)`. This ranks genes within each specimen. It is relative transcriptomic abundance, not absolute expression or a patient-population percentile. Freeze exact gene IDs, annotation releases, ties, duplicate/missing rules and coverage; all required P/U measurements must be available under the accepted policy. An unrelated change in the rank universe can change Y. Whole-transcriptome rank transport across RNA-seq and two microarray platforms remains a measurement assumption, not something guaranteed by matching gene symbols.

Q must specify cognate transcript/TSS, build, strand/window, source mask, shared-probe handling and aggregation. Manufacturer TSS200/TSS1500 categories are not identical to a generic ±1500-bp window. HLA probe specificity and shared TAP/PSMB region membership need explicit review. Do not count shared probes as independent regulatory observations. Outcome-dependent probe selection is prohibited.

Where CpG coverage is learned from data, fit it inside every applicable training fold. Existing fold-local code does this in its fixture path; preserve that interface. Fit scalers and ridge tuning separately within TCGA training partitions; retain identical validation patients for baseline and extended models. Freeze final TCGA Q, transforms, coefficients and eligibility rules before external scoring.

`Delta_R2=(SSE_baseline-SSE_extended)/SST`, where both errors and SST use the identical eligible external patient set and Y definition. Report negative values. For n<2 or constant Y, report undefined with reason rather than zero. A fixed-model bootstrap quantifies external-sample uncertainty conditional on the fitted models, not uncertainty of repeating model development.

## Critical B-P gates

CPC's 210 historical patient-code matches are not final N. The stronger 73 original-study bridges and 72 available Qpure values are also pre-QC coverage, not eligible analysis counts. Qpure and TCGA ABSOLUTE are different estimators; a common 0–1 scale is insufficient comparability evidence. The portal WGS_BASED_PURITY_ESTIMATION field is prohibited because the repository's original-source audit identifies it as SNP-call agreement, not tumour purity.

The earlier complete CPC expression coverage audit exposed source values before final scientific freeze; it reported no computed Y or associations. Record that history and any other inherited analyses. Do not claim untouched prospective blindness solely because this review has not scored CPC. Any external-dependent gene selection invalidates the intended independent role.

Do not choose a minimum meaningful Delta_R2 or adequate N from desired outcomes. Before unlocking external Y, justify an assay-relevant improvement/precision target using the score scale, measurement reliability and prespecified hypothetical scenarios; exact threshold remains UNKNOWN. Restriction of external variation can make SST and Delta_R2 imprecise. If comparable baseline/score/precision cannot be defended, B-P stays blocked pending an explicit amendment, not a hidden fallback.

## Common inference rules

Continuous programme analyses are preferred; no median split by default. A score-derived group cannot independently validate its own component genes. Use source-native gene scales for gene associations and declared cross-platform effect synthesis, rather than joint outcome-informed normalisation. Across-patient empirical rank regression is distinct from fixed within-sample scoring and needs rank-aware inference; do not revive the withdrawn B-R HC3 shortcut.

Missing expression, methylation and clinical history are not zero. Complete-case estimates apply to their eligible populations; record exclusions and compare available/unavailable subsets. Any imputation must be separately justified and training-only for prediction. Do not impute unknown treatment as no treatment.

Preserve nulls, opposing effects, assay noncoverage and non-estimable models. For meta-analysis, require comparable estimands and account for patient/study overlap; a random-effects model does not repair perfect disease/platform confounding. Survival is not in the core contract: it needs a separately frozen endpoint, time origin, event definition, censoring assumptions and precision rationale before use.
