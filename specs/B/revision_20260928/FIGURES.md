# Revised four-figure panel contract

PROPOSED. Every panel exports a patient-safe plotted table with source/analysis/contract hashes, actual eligible N and an explanation of both positive and null results. No mock data may appear as biological findings.

| Panel | Question | Source and N/unit | Analysis and uncertainty | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | How does clinical response evidence enter B? | Frozen A originating studies; patients and studies separately counted | Evidence-flow diagram plus actual admission counts; no statistical interval for deterministic counts | Clinical anchor provenance, not proof of cross-cancer transport |
| 1B | Which patients and assays support each analysis? | TCGA/CPC/qualified sources; patient, specimen and assay N | Flow and modality-intersection/missingness display; exact counts | Eligible populations and overlap accountability |
| 1C | What clinical states were sampled? | Source-backed stage/grade/site/treatment fields; patients | Distributions with UNKNOWN visible; denominators per field | Cohort context, not population prevalence |
| 2A | Which A programmes differ in declared prostate contrasts? | Verified paired prostate samples and separate PRAD/LUAD population | B01/B02 effect forest and distributions; CI and predeclared multiplicity | Relative expression alteration, not functional deficit |
| 2B | Could bulk differences reflect mixture? | Same eligible RNA/non-RNA purity/composition subset | Unadjusted and predeclared sensitivity effects on identical patients; CI | Sensitivity to composition assumptions, not proof of confounding or tumour causality |
| 2C | Does state modify a programme relationship? | Qualified prostate clinical populations; patient grouped | Continuous association or explicit interaction with CI | Within-cohort state association |
| 3A | Which methylation relationships support or contradict the hypothesis? | Discovery linked RNA/methylation; patients and CpGs separately labelled | B03 signed effect/CI with coverage and shared-locus flags | Regulatory association, not silencing |
| 3B | Do genetic/chromatin/protein alternatives change interpretation? | Same-specimen subsets or explicitly separate corroborating cohorts | Matched-patient CNA sensitivity; actual analyte effects/CI; optional MOFA only if informative | Competing explanation or orthogonal corroboration |
| 3C | Which associations replicate? | Independently qualified prostate cohort; patient N | Frozen B04 family, native-scale estimates/CI, multiplicity, opposing results | Replication only of the specific estimand/population |
| 4A | Does promoter methylation improve the fixed APM prediction externally? | Locked CPC B-P eligible patients | Observed/predicted; individual R2, paired Delta_R2, CI, SSE/SST/N, undefined results | External molecular prediction, not ICI sensitivity |
| 4B | What evidence supports each frozen nominee? | Freeze D candidate rows, external annotations appended | Nonweighted evidence matrix: support/opposition/inconclusive/unmeasured; numerical effects linked | Testable hypotheses with explicit uncertainty |
| 4C | What can C/wet lab test next? | Frozen handoff and actual assay/model coverage | Evidence-stage diagram/readout table; no invented effect sizes | Nomination versus intervention and functional validation boundaries |

Cellular or spatial evidence belongs in 3B/4B only when it resolves origin/exclusion and passes donor-aware gates. Otherwise retain a visible missing-evidence field rather than adding a weak decorative panel. If panel density becomes excessive, move detailed calibration/coverage and exploratory factors to supplements, not the core limitations or negative primary result.
