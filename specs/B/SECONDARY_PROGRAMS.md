## Approved-immunotherapy drug targets

The user explicitly requested the broader targets of approved immunotherapy drugs. Include a separate exploratory drug-target registry, not a merged all-positive immune score. Required fields: drug, regulatory authority, approval/label source and date, indication, modality, molecular target and canonical gene, mechanism/direction, tumor versus immune-cell context, assay coverage and inclusion/exclusion reason. Distinguish approved targets from investigational targets and drug-binding targets from response biomarkers.

Sol proposed an FDA/EMA solid-tumor immunomodulatory subset. Its exclusions of cell therapies, bispecific tumor engagers, tumor-directed antibodies and other classes are a proposed curation boundary, not an explicit user-approved narrowing. Retain those categories in the scope audit with their disposition rather than silently dropping the broader request. Freeze the exact regulatory cutoff and included categories before calling the registry complete.
