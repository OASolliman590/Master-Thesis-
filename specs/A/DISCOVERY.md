# A retained responder-signature discovery module

Status: full conditional design proposal. This restores an implementable branch for the submitted cross-cancer responder-signature task. It is not approved, cancelled, or satisfied by a fixed CYT analysis. Numerical settings below are proposed design choices; no performance or cohort coverage is asserted.

## Question, endpoint and competing primary choice

Learn a regularised transcriptomic signature of objective response to ICI in source cancers, test it on independent originating studies, then pass a frozen gene/coefficient product to prostate characterization and B/C. Outcome Y=1 means documented best overall CR/PR (including explicitly combined PRCR); Y=0 means documented SD/PD. An unspecified binary responder label or a PSA endpoint is not automatically this outcome. Different assessment systems/treatment timelines must pass DATA.md harmonization before admission.

**A-P2, competing proposed primary:** for a locked discovered score f and fixed CYT score s, estimate D_j=AUC_j(f,Y_ORR)−AUC_j(s,Y_ORR) on identical eligible baseline patients in each untouched final-test cohort j. Aggregate by equal cancer weights and equal cohort weights within cancer, as in ANALYSIS.md, using a separately frozen discovery-test manifest. The target is incremental retrospective discrimination of the locked score in that finite independent study collection, conditional on its development data. It is not predictive treatment benefit versus a non-ICI control. Exactly one of A-P1 and A-P2 must become the registered primary; the unchosen analysis remains a declared secondary module. No result may decide the choice.

If independent final-test data cannot be established, cohort-held-out development estimates remain reportable as internal-external validation of the learning procedure; they cannot silently replace A-P2's independent-test estimand. Document a reviewed endpoint amendment or leave that primary nonestimable.

## Source admission and development/test separation

DATA.md contains current source ceilings; no cohort is admitted yet. GSE91061 and GSE176307 have candidate objective-response categories. GSE78220 may support ORR if original source definitions, baseline status and patient uniqueness are verified even though it does not inform the SD-switch primary. GSE126044 can enter only if the original binary categories are proved equivalent to the declared ORR definition; GSE135222's inspected PFS fields do not qualify. Existing ICBatlas/IOhub/TIGER and old-run cohort lists are discovery catalogues, not additional independent patients. Audit original accessions, trial IDs, duplicate subjects, score-development overlap, units and available matrices before adding a source. There is no inferred1,300-patient eligible set.

The proposed tuned design requires at least three independent development cohorts, each supplying both outcome classes and an assay-compatible feature universe, so every outer leave-cohort-out development split can contain at least two source cohorts for inner tuning. This is a split-feasibility condition, not sufficient power. Reserve one or more independently sourced final-test cohorts before feature exploration; any prior outcome/model exposure is disclosed and may prevent claiming untouched confirmation. No such split is presently verified. Leave-cancer-out evaluation additionally requires that every proposed held-out cancer leaves an estimable training/tuning structure. If not, report that transport test unavailable rather than substitute patient-random folds.

## Proposed learner and training-only processing

1. Admit baseline RNA abundance only after exact annotation/TPM or valid full-universe FPKM-to-TPM conversion is verified. No array-to-TPM fabrication. Raw count inputs need a separately reviewed quantification/normalization contract before admission. Use a predeclared common annotation universe from assay documentation; outcome values do not determine it.
2. Within each **inner training** partition, apply log2(1+TPM). Retain genes with TPM≥1 in at least20% of that partition's patients and nonzero training variance. Retain at most5,000 by training variance, breaking ties lexicographically by canonical gene ID. These thresholds/cap remain proposed; do not tune them using outer or final-test outcomes. Exclude genes with unresolved assay/mapping or missing values under the frozen source contract; do not treat unmeasured genes as zeros. If a held-out assay cannot supply selected features, that fold is nonestimable unless a missing-feature strategy was separately frozen before evaluation.
3. Fit per-gene mean and population standard deviation on the inner training partition only, then reuse them for its validation partition. Do not center/ComBat jointly with held-out cohorts or fit a separate test-cohort standardizer. Each outer fit reruns this entire transformation using only its outer training cohorts.
4. Fit binary elastic-net logistic regression with intercept, SAGA solver, no automatic class balancing, and a prespecified grid C∈{0.01,0.1,1,10}, l1_ratio∈{0.1,0.5,0.9}. Training sample weight is proportional to1/(number of cancers × cohorts in that cancer × patients in that cohort), normalized to sum to training N. This controls cohort contribution; it does not eliminate cancer/treatment confounding. Set proposed random seed20260905, tolerance1e−4 and max_iter10,000; convergence failures are recorded, not accepted as completed fits.
5. Select hyperparameters by equal-cancer/equal-cohort mean **inner held-out** AUROC. Resolve exact ties by smaller C, then larger l1_ratio. A candidate must be estimable/converged on every frozen inner fold; if none qualifies, fail the outer fit. Freeze all settings before outer/final outcomes are evaluated.

The inspected scikit-learn1.9 API supports SAGA with elastic-net mixing through `l1_ratio` and `C`; its `penalty` parameter is deprecated. Use actual versioned API behavior, not an old template with guessed defaults. [LogisticRegression1.9 documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/1.9/modules/generated/sklearn.linear_model.LogisticRegression.html), checked5 September2026. Cohort/cancer fold indices must be explicit and patient grouped; [LeaveOneGroupOut documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/1.9/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.LeaveOneGroupOut.html) supplies a splitting primitive, not the nested pipeline by itself.

## Validation, uncertainty and comparators

- Development: outer leave-one-originating-cohort-out; inner leave-cohort-out tuning among remaining development cohorts. Save patient-level outer predictions and every transform/hyperparameter manifest. Separately run leave-cancer-out only where the stated split gates pass. Do not call held-out patients from the same cohort external transport.
- Fixed comparator: the documented CYT score, higher predicting response. Supporting comparators: an intercept-only predictor and a logistic model using log2(1+CYT), trained/tuned only within the same development partitions. Use identical patient sets and disclose when a feature gate makes a comparison nonestimable.
- Final test: tune/refit the specified pipeline on all admitted development cohorts, freeze its coefficients/transforms and apply once to the reserved studies. No final-test threshold optimization, sign reversal, recalibration or gene replacement. Discrimination is the primary metric; optional calibration requires probabilities and is a separately declared analysis.
- A-P2 interval:2,000 paired patient bootstrap replicates within each final-test cohort's O versus non-O categories, preserving the two scores per patient and fixed cancer/cohort weights. Use the percentile0.025/0.975 interval with `method="linear"` and recorded PCG64 seed. This estimates test-sample uncertainty conditional on the fitted training model and observed category composition; it does not measure uncertainty of retraining on new development cohorts. Never use a patient bootstrap over pooled outer predictions as if overlapping training fits were independent.
- Report development performance by held-out study/cancer and feature stability descriptively. If learning-procedure confidence intervals are required, a separate resampling contract that reruns selection/tuning and respects source clusters must be reviewed before execution; it is not required or implied by the conditional A-P2 interval. Precision of the independent-test contrast must be evaluated from admitted class counts before the primary choice is frozen.

Failure of any frozen required final-test cohort leaves the primary incomplete; do not renormalize surviving studies. No observed AUROC threshold is an acceptance criterion.

## Product passed to prostate, B and C

Export all retained candidate coefficients, canonical gene IDs, training-only filters/scalers, sign, rank by absolute coefficient, model version, source-cohort list, exposure ledger and foldwise sign/nonzero frequency. A positive coefficient denotes higher fitted response score with greater standardized expression, not proof of an immune mechanism. Rank all genes; do not force a fixed number of immune genes or require canonical recovery. Export a separate source-supported immune annotation without deleting contrary genes.

The operational handoff uses the all-development refit frozen **before final-test inspection**. Outer-fold summaries describe stability but cannot justify post-test feature choice. B/C may intersect this prespecified product with their measured assays under their own contracts; report missing features and preserve version provenance. Primary model predictions from the frozen coefficients must not be confused with an unweighted truncated gene-list score. A null or nontransporting signature remains a valid product.

Acceptance gates: verified source definitions and matrices; sufficient independent split structure; exposure/final-test separation; source-correct feature mapping; frozen learner and primary choice; meaningful precision assessment; leakage/oracle/convergence tests; real AIU tracer; reviewed B/C handoff. None is currently claimed satisfied merely by writing this module.
