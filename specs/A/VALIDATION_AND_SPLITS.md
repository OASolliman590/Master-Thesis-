# Validation, splits and prior exposure

## Protected unit

Assign roles using the originating study and its overlap-connected family, with patient-level exclusion across aliases, technical replicates, lesions, longitudinal specimens and modalities. The final-test reservation applies to all related material across A-BIO, A-PRED, A1 and B/C. Splits are generated from the shared authoritative registry, not independently by each analysis. A nested fold assignment is scoped to its model/evaluation; it must not overwrite a patient's permanent development/final-test role.

## Exposure categories

Track metadata-only/clinical curation separately from molecular-outcome inspection, DEG/module construction, predictor tuning, prior testing, atlas-derived feature use and comparator development. Endpoint curation and class-count precision planning are necessary and permitted; they do not automatically equal predictive training. Unknown historical exposure is UNKNOWN, not untouched. If a catalogue's outcome-derived genes entered development, its originating patients are exposed even if only a mirror was read. Moving files/repositories or re-pseudonymizing IDs never restores independence.

Use honest labels: UNTOUCHED_FINAL_TEST; PREVIOUSLY_INSPECTED_EXTERNAL; INTERNAL_EXTERNAL; RETROSPECTIVE_TRANSPORT; DEVELOPMENT; UNKNOWN; EXCLUDED. Method-specific published-comparator training overlap must be recorded separately. A study may be unseen for our learner but not for a comparator, limiting comparative interpretation.

## Information barriers

Before A-BIO exploration, reserve potential confirmation families and lock an exposure snapshot. Curators can reconstruct endpoints/headers, but the development role cannot inspect protected expression–response relationships, source DEG tables, test predictions or A1 changes from those families. Document who accessed which object/field and why. Final-test header/annotation coverage checks do not authorize data-driven feature repair. Leakage mutation tests must prove that modifying final-test expression/labels cannot change development features, tuning, transforms or frozen handoff hashes.

## Precision planning

Use admitted class counts, exact overlap structure, compatible feature availability and declared simulation assumptions; label simulations as hypothetical, not observed results. Vary plausible discriminations and paired-score correlations rather than assume success. The user/statistical reviewer selects a material precision target before final evaluation. Three development cohorts or two outcome classes establish only algebraic feasibility, not precision. No outcome-associated model performance can choose the test roster or stop recruitment/search opportunistically.

## Branch-specific failure

Without a qualifying untouched test, the selected A-P2 independent evaluation is nonestimable; development/previously inspected validation may be reported under its correct label after separate release. A-BIO, fixed-CYT A-P1 and A1 can continue with their own admissible populations and protected-set exclusions. A failed paired modality blocks only that corroboration. A failed required frozen source makes its aggregate incomplete; changes require a dated prospectively justified amendment, not a post-result deletion.

## Verification records

A split receipt pins overlap graph hash, prior-exposure ledger hash, study-family roles, patient membership hash, curator/reviewer, data/version timestamps and blocked reasons. The private validation suite asserts family/patient disjointness, treatment-episode integrity, row-order invariance, role consistency and absence of private IDs in public reports. No cohort or patient split is instantiated by this specification package.
