Do not invent their counts or bibliographic metadata.

## Candidate new knowledge

The proposed contribution is a separation of reproducible response biology from predictive coefficients, with explicit assay/effect scales, tumour-composition alternatives, cancer/regimen heterogeneity and a frozen clinical-reference handoff for prostate epigenetic investigation. A1 adds direct response-associated differential change, with exact treatment context and biopsy-selection limitations. These are hypotheses about publishable contribution, not guaranteed novelty.

Publication evaluation must answer: does the analysis change what is known about robust versus context-dependent programmes; is independence real rather than a new database label; is uncertainty informative even for a null result; and does the B/C handoff avoid translating predictive weights into restoration targets? An audit-quality data resource can be valuable but is not automatically a biological discovery manuscript.

## Required update artifact

Maintain closest_studies.tsv with exact query/date/source, originating trial, public data objects, endpoint, temporal design, assay, training/evaluation families, overlap with our roster, method availability, unresolved supplement questions and contribution overlap. Record publication dates separately from data/recruitment dates. At scientific freeze, reviewer signs the comparison and limitations; at submission rerun the update search. A missing comparison blocks the corresponding novelty claim, not source-parser development.
