# Prospective wet-lab handoff

## Current protocol anchor

The internal protocol proposes a mechanism-matched funnel: tumour-model qualification, drug target engagement, washout/recovery, molecular restoration, and HLA-restricted tumour–T-cell killing with a priming × anti-PD-1 interaction. The preferred functional endpoint is viable tumour loss at a prespecified timepoint, supported by apoptosis, degranulation, cytokine, and exhaustion readouts.

## Current reality requiring reconciliation

User-reported status includes PC3 under ordering, no animals, human immune cells planned, research-grade pembrolizumab/ipilimumab/relatlimab acquired, atezolizumab ordered, azacitidine available, and possible access to commercial vorinostat/ricolinostat/azacitidine tablets. The procurement document lists T-cell activator, histone-acetylation antibodies, IL-2, IFN-gamma, Fluo-4, PD-L1 flow antibody, CD4/CD8 reagents, and Fc block. None of these statements alone proves receipt, lot quality, functional blocking, sterility, HLA compatibility, or assay readiness.

## Required sequence

1. inventory and lot reconciliation;
2. tumour/immune/HLA/antigen qualification;
3. dose and target-engagement calibration;
4. model calibration experiments;
5. frozen simulation predictions;
6. separate prospective evaluation experiments;
7. discrepancy report before model update.

## Handoff packet

Every prediction specifies cell model, immune source, intervention identity, dose, sequence, timepoints, predicted molecular/protein/functional changes, controls, assay, meaningful effect, interval, null expectation, falsifying outcome, and alternative explanations.

Antibodies are not tested as tumour-monoculture efficacy agents. An ICI-success claim requires a qualified immune-functional context.
