# Accepted decisions D-A through D-H

## D-A — historical D1 primary

**Accepted with modification.** Preserve the exact conditional D1 task. D1 is not an unavoidable prerequisite for measured-driver D2-PAIR.

## D-B — D2-lite

**Accepted.** Start with a qualified tumour–T-cell pair model. Myeloid, fibroblast, endothelial, B-cell/TLS, recruitment, and additional spatial biology are conditional extensions.

## D-C — digital twin and Paper E

**Accepted.** Defer patient-specific digital twins and individualized clinical recommendations. This does not defer Paper E. D and E have distinct responsibilities and a formal interface.

## D-D — primary promotion

**Accepted.** A D2 primary requires an explicit amendment before confirmatory outcome inspection. Outcome-informed selection is exploratory and requires new validation.

## D-E — data access

**Accepted with limits.** Public and appropriately approved project data are allowed. Controlled access, author contact, paid transfer, account creation, and new terms acceptance require separate authorization.

## D-F — model scope

**Accepted.** Audit STATE first. Tahoe-x1 and PIE are conditional. No large foundation-model training.

## D-G — prospective experiment family

**Accepted as a family, not frozen settings.** One epigenetic agent × one qualified checkpoint/immune-system contrast is planned after inventory, assay, HLA/antigen, dose, timing, and replication qualification.

## D-H — specification and repository action

**Accepted.** Preserve historical D, integrate this dated revision through a review branch and draft PR, and do not merge automatically.

## Binding claim limits

- No causal-control claim from a simulated transition alone.
- No ICI-success claim from tumour monoculture or gene markers alone.
- No B/TLS output when the model lacks B/TLS representation.
- No patient-level prediction from cohort archetypes.
- No independent-test claim with unknown training overlap.
- No synergy claim without a declared null and matched measured combination data.
