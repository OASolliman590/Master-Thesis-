# Immune-checkpoint inhibitor mechanism contract

ICI is a multicellular interaction modifier, not an ordinary tumour-cell perturbation token.

## Initial anti-PD-1/PD-L1 representation

Represent only when:

- effector cells express/engage the receptor pathway in the assay;
- tumour or relevant partner cells express the ligand in the assay;
- the antibody/blocking reagent is functionally qualified;
- matched isotype/no-antibody controls exist;
- receptor occupancy/detection interference is considered.

Candidate model actions include altered probability or duration of productive contact, reduced checkpoint-mediated inhibition, restored killing, or improved immune persistence. Each action is a calibrated parameter with uncertainty, not an assumed universal multiplier.

## Other ICIs

Anti-CTLA-4 and anti-LAG-3 require separate mechanisms and suitable cellular contexts. They are not assigned the anti-PD-1 parameter. Ipilimumab is not represented in a minimal tumour–CD8 system as though its priming/Treg/Fc mechanisms were present.

## Claim ceiling

A model can support a prediction for the qualified assay. It cannot establish clinical benefit or interchangeability among checkpoint antibodies.
