# Paper D approved revision — 2026-10-15

**Status:** approved scientific specification; implementation remains gated.  
**Historical D1 primary:** preserved.  
**Initial multicellular architecture:** D2-PAIR, measured-driver tumour–T-cell model.  
**Forbidden claim:** validated patient-specific digital twin.

## Scientific question

The staged programme asks two separately identifiable questions:

1. **D1:** Can a frozen virtual-cell perturbation model predict independently measured, checkpoint-compatible prostate chemical responses more accurately than the fixed same-drug response baseline?
2. **D2-PAIR:** Can a calibrated, minimal tumour–T-cell model predict an independent functional outcome better than a nonspatial count model and a minimal rule-only spatial baseline?

Learned molecular coupling is evaluated only after both the molecular model and the molecular-to-phenotype bridge pass their own gates.

## Minimal viable paper

A publishable minimum is one of the following, declared prospectively:

- a completed and informative D1 benchmark, including a negative result, with strong baselines and failure analysis; or
- a validated D2-PAIR paper with measured drivers, independent functional ground truth, strong nonspatial/rule baselines, and a prospective prediction; or
- a staged paper containing both, with no claim that one validates the other.

The title must reflect the highest completed validation level. “Virtual TME” is acceptable only when multicellular/spatial components have been implemented and validated; “digital twin” is prohibited under the current scope.
