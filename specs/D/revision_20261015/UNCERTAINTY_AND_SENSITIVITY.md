# Uncertainty and sensitivity

Separate and report:

- measurement uncertainty;
- biological heterogeneity;
- parameter uncertainty;
- stochastic simulation uncertainty;
- foundation-model seed uncertainty;
- structural uncertainty;
- domain-shift uncertainty;
- source/ontology mapping uncertainty.

Cells are nested measurements, not population replicates. Use donor/experiment/plate-aware intervals. Report prediction intervals or ensemble ranges where identifiable. Do not publish a single deterministic trajectory as a patient prediction.

Global sensitivity analysis must precede biological interpretation of uncertain mappings. Structural alternatives—such as contact-rate versus killing-probability explanations—are compared explicitly rather than hidden in one fitted coefficient.
