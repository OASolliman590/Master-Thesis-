# Combination and sequence contract

## Bounded initial scenarios

1. vehicle/isotype;
2. epigenetic agent alone;
3. qualified checkpoint blockade alone;
4. concurrent combination;
5. epigenetic priming then checkpoint blockade;
6. reverse sequence only when biologically/experimentally supportable.

Do not simulate the full Cartesian product of drugs, doses, schedules, and cell systems.

## Scenario record

Each scenario carries compounds, targets, identities, doses, times, sequence, context, measured/calibrated inputs, assumptions, outputs, comparator, validation status, and claim ceiling.

## Synergy

A simulation ranking is not synergy evidence. A synergy claim requires a declared null (for example Bliss/HSA/ZIP or a mechanistically justified interaction model), matched doses/timing, independent measured combination data, and appropriate uncertainty. The core wet-lab functional question may instead be the preregistered epigenetic-priming × anti-PD-1 interaction.
