## Programme constraints

- one accountable integrator;
- no more than two independent child workers initially;
- one writer owns each registry/module;
- no large/model/controlled-data work without release;
- technical fixtures do not validate biology;
- null and negative results remain valid outputs;
- no ticket closes the historical Paper D issue unless its own acceptance criteria and the parent criteria are met.
