# Protocol and thesis role

Paper D is an optional computational–experimental modelling chapter that connects, but does not replace, the other thesis papers.

| Paper | Owns | Paper D may consume | Paper D must not duplicate |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | pretreatment ICI response programmes and clinical effect estimates | frozen programme definitions | clinical predictor discovery/validation |
| A.1 | observed longitudinal response-by-time effects and transitions | frozen observed transition targets | observational longitudinal analysis |
| B | prostate immune deficits and epigenetic/regulatory evidence | prostate archetypes and reversible-barrier candidates | methylation/regulatory inference |
| C | measured perturbation evidence and drug prioritisation | evidence-constrained intervention shortlist | drug nomination from simulation alone |
| D | virtual perturbation benchmarking; spatial/contact modelling; counterfactual hypotheses | frozen inputs from A/B/C/E and wet lab | measured biology or clinical benefit claims |
| E | PBPK/PKPD–QSP, exposure, induction/recovery, virtual populations, schedule optimisation | frozen exposure or PD trajectories | spatial/contact tumour–immune modelling |
| Wet-lab paper | molecular restoration, protein confirmation, immune interaction and killing | prospective test outcomes | replacement by simulation |

The registered protocol includes cell-model selection, epigenetic treatment, qPCR/Western readouts, and potential functional extensions. Paper D strengthens the computational interface but cannot be counted as experimental completion.
