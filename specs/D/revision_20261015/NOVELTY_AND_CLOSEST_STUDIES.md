# Novelty and closest studies

## Closest precedent classes

1. Longitudinal pan-cancer TIME-state studies, including Lin et al.
2. Virtual-cell perturbation models such as STATE and candidate Tahoe-x1/PIE systems.
3. Strong perturbation baselines and benchmark critiques such as Systema and linear baseline studies.
4. PhysiCell/PhysiBoSS tumour–immune simulations.
5. Hybrid ML + agent-based or QSP models.
6. Epigenetic-drug + ICI experimental literature in prostate and other cancers.

## What is not novel

- reproducing Lin et al.;
- using STATE, Tahoe, PhysiCell, or PhysiBoSS;
- defining another static inflamed/myeloid state;
- showing that on-treatment changes matter;
- creating a marker list;
- simulating an epigenetic drug + ICI combination.

## Candidate contribution

A defensible contribution requires at least one independently validated addition:

- a prostate-context reliability test of a released perturbation model against information-matched baselines;
- a measured-driver tumour–T-cell model that predicts held-out functional outcomes;
- a prospective test showing where learned molecular predictions improve or fail to improve multicellular prediction;
- an empirically anchored state-transition hypothesis with explicit uncertainty and assay falsification;
- evidence that spatial/contact modelling adds value over a nonspatial baseline.

Novelty is re-evaluated after the final source, model, and validation dataset are selected. No absence-of-precedent claim is frozen now.
