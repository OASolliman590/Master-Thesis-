# Four-figure architecture

## Figure 1 — foundation and information boundaries

Lin/TIME observer role, prostate mapping, A/B/C/E interfaces, source coverage, training/development/test separation, and D1/D2-PAIR architecture.

## Figure 2 — D1 molecular benchmark

Eligibility/overlap, STATE/candidate model versus the fixed same-drug baseline and secondary baselines, perturbation discrimination, positive-control/data-ceiling, and failure cases.

## Figure 3 — D2-PAIR validation

Experimental geometry, measured parameter/calibration flow, nonspatial versus rule-only versus calibrated spatial model, predicted versus held-out tumour burden/killing, uncertainty, and identifiability.

## Figure 4 — bounded sequence hypotheses and prospective test

Small scenario registry, archetype/context dependence, frozen prediction packet, tumour/immune/response/failure panels, prospective result/discrepancy, and explicit claim ceiling.

Every panel declares question, source, biological unit, model, comparator, uncertainty, validation level, and permitted conclusion. Result figures are not generated until the corresponding analyses are released.
