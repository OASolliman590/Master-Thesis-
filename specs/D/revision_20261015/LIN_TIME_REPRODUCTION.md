# Lin et al. TIME reproduction and extension contract

## Role

Lin et al. supplies a candidate TME-state ontology, observed favourable/unfavourable transitions, processed single-cell/spatial objects, and an external response-associated signature. Reproduction is a foundation and benchmark—not Paper D novelty.

## Source-resolution tiers

### L0 — source and rights

Resolve final article, complete supplement, processed objects, source-study composition, code availability, licences, redistribution limits, and controlled/public boundaries.

### L1 — object inventory

Reproduce patients, samples, cancer types, timepoints, regimens, longitudinal pairs, cell counts, cell labels, Visium samples, and missingness.

### L2 — state observer

Reconstruct or approximate the published hierarchical cell annotation and four TIME-state assignment. Record exact code/methods, modifications, agreement metrics, and unresolved divergences.

### L3 — transitions

Reproduce baseline/follow-up state probabilities, transition labels/matrix, response associations, and patient-level unit handling.

### L4 — spatial and bulk corroboration

Reproduce only source-supported spatial neighbourhood/projection and bulk signature analyses with declared independence boundaries.

## Fail-closed rules

- Missing supplement/code does not permit guessed methods.
- Reproduction failure blocks Lin-dependent observer/transition claims only.
- Lin source data used to derive an observer cannot be called independent validation of that observer.
- The 59-gene signature is external comparison material unless its derivation and splits are fully resolved.
- Licence uncertainty blocks redistribution, not necessarily private metadata inspection where lawful.
