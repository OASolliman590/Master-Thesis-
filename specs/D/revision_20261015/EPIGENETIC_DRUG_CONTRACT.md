# Epigenetic-drug contract

## Representation

Every intervention specifies compound identity, formulation/source, target class, cell context, concentration, duration, sequence, washout/recovery, measured target engagement, viability, programme/protein/function effects, and uncertainty.

## Critical caution

Epigenetic drugs may activate tumour immunogenicity, impair immune cells, or cause nonspecific cytotoxic/stress effects. No uniformly immune-activating prior is permitted.

## Current candidate/inventory reconciliation

The scientific protocol discusses decitabine, entinostat, and tazemetostat. User-reported availability includes azacitidine and access to commercial vorinostat/ricolinostat/azacitidine formulations; formulation suitability, identity, potency, sterility, excipients, and target engagement require qualification. Final D-G compound selection is therefore **not frozen**.

## Calibration gate

A regimen enters D2 only after:

- post-washout viability and direct toxicity are quantified;
- class-specific target engagement is shown;
- timing and recovery are measured;
- the exact tumour and immune contexts are defined;
- calibration and prospective evaluation experiments are separated.
