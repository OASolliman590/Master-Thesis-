# Scientific figures and their evidence requirements

No biological results or synthetic biology figures are generated by this specification. Future simulated trajectories are explicitly labelled model outputs; illustrative fixtures cannot appear as measured results.

| Figure/panel | Question and source | Analysis/comparator | N/uncertainty/status/claim |
|---|---|---|---|

| 4A robustness | What changes the ranking? Evidence deletion, parameters, structure, correlations | Prespecified alternative structures and uncertainty sources | Conditional sensitivity, not independent biological validation |

Tables: source-by-question/access/licence; lead selection; parameter provenance/identifiability; original-study/data-role overlap; validation level; primary contrast/assumptions; claims and unvalidated domains. Every panel receipt records source object IDs, role, original N/unknown-N reason, analysis/code/run hashes, comparator, uncertainty, current status and permitted conclusion.

C-IDENTIFIABILITY is not allowed to hide lack of independent measured schedule testing; its figure 3B must show the actual ambiguity/certificate. A biological validation figure is reserved for a future E_LAST version and disclosed as such.
