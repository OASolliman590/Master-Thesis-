# What the revised paper asks

The public-data paper asks whether existing measurements let us distinguish useful treatment timings, and how much the answer depends on unmeasured tumour exposure, recovery or immune impairment. It does not ask our laboratory to finish experiments before the computational paper can close.

Exposure asks how much drug reaches the measured compartment over time. Priming PD asks what the source assay shows during and after exposure. Reduced QSP asks how exposure, tumour properties, immune function and blockade could jointly affect killing. Ensembles ask whether the conclusion survives all supported alternatives, rather than whether one fitted curve looks favourable.

Public literature does contain relevant experiments (S001, S004, V201), but those observations must be used on their actual scale, for their actual drug and cell context. Missing links stay unknown. A credible result may be that timing cannot be inferred from the available data, demonstrated quantitatively.

Later, E_LAST can test or revise the model using our measurements. No local data means no E_LAST execution; it does not reopen a scientifically accepted public-core release. The degree's biological requirements remain a separate issue.
