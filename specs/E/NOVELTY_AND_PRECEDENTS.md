A search also surfaced a 2026 adaptive prostate-QSP scheduling preprint lead, DOI `10.64898/2026.06.09.730783`, from secondary indexes. The primary DOI route could not be accessed in this revision. It remains an **unverified novelty lead**, not a cited method or a basis for factual claims. Resolve its primary text/version/code before asserting novelty against it.

Reuse categories: exact reproduction only with original recoverable assets and parity; partial reconstruction with a documented gap list; independent reimplementation with mathematical/source mapping; new model with a distinct structure/observation contract. Licence and numerical parity are separate checks. Open article text does not prove open runnable model files or an open training dataset.

Candidate novelty is a measurement-linked non-identifiability/robustness result across exposure, delayed priming and adverse immune effects, with correct data-role independence and reusable public provenance. It is not a promise of acceptance or a claim that all such analysis is unprecedented.
