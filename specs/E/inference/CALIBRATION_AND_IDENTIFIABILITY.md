# Calibration, evidence constraints and identifiable questions

## Role freeze

Before real fitting, freeze original studies/publication families, actual objects, development/model-selection/evaluation roles, selected compound/context, observation transforms and primary contrast. A paper, its supplement, regulatory submission and database mirror may represent the same participants/controls. Group at the highest shared biological source level appropriate to the claim. Never split curve points or wells to create independence.

## Sequential inference with propagated uncertainty

First fit/reconstruct systemic exposure. Carry its parameter samples or feasible region into PD, then into functional QSP, preserving dependence. Modular fitting may use a cut-feedback approach so unsupported tumour outcomes cannot retune systemic PK; document the factorisation and any feedback restriction. Joint fitting is allowed only with justified likelihoods and overlap accounting. Do not pass only best-fit exposure into downstream uncertainty.

Positive individual concentrations can use lognormal/proportional errors plus a justified additive component. Aggregate means require their actual arithmetic/geometric/median definition and known sampling errors; unknown SD/SE remains unknown. Digitised observations include extraction error. A summary and its underlying curve are not independent likelihood terms. Censored values use the documented assay limit and likelihood or explicit interval constraint.

## Identifiability workflow

List free/fixed/derived/prior/unresolved parameters and observable-state mappings. Run structural assessment on the selected reduced system, sensitivity rank/parameter-combination analysis, multistart and profile likelihood or adequately diagnosed posterior inference. Diagnose practical non-identifiability with interval widths/multimodality and prior dependence. Reparameterise identifiable products/ratios rather than impose narrow arbitrary priors.

Compare direct/no-delay, exposure-only, turnover and qualified alternatives on the same data with equal tuning opportunity. Do not reward complexity by giving it extra source measurements or test-set tuning. Parameter identifiability and prediction identifiability are separate: profile the primary Delta itself.

## C-IDENTIFIABILITY certificate

Provide source-data hashes and the full observation mapping, an equivalence/profiling argument, feasible parameter/structure witnesses, their residuals under the frozen adequacy criteria, and the resulting primary contrasts. Show that any sign disagreement is not numerical instability or unequal exposure. If Delta remains stable despite uncertain parameters, report that stability instead of claiming timing is unknowable.

An interval bound can be infinite or uncomputed. A failed optimiser cannot establish non-identifiability; a successful solver cannot establish identifiability. For numerical certificates, preserve multistart seeds, convergence checks and unresolved global-coverage limitations. Label assumptions controlling feasibility and perform evidence/assumption deletion analyses.

## Completion

Core real calibration uses public admitted objects only. No donor count or future HLA measurement is a release condition. Independent public evaluation is reported by level; future original validation belongs to a distinct model version and specification.
