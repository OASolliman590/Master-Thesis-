# Uncertainty propagation and claim constraints

Maintain separate labels for assay measurement error, public-summary sampling uncertainty, digitisation error, experimental/context variability, parameter uncertainty, model structure, compartment/domain transfer and numerical approximation. A single wide ribbon conceals why predictions differ; attribute the dominant sources as well as show a combined conditional envelope.

Propagate PK uncertainty into site exposure and PD/QSP without fitting tumour partitioning to force benefit. Include alternative priming persistence, no-delay/no-priming, adverse immune effects, checkpoint sensitivity and compensatory mechanisms when not excluded. Investigate free plasma-to-medium/intracellular bridges explicitly. A cell-culture nominal concentration equal to human Cmax does not prove safe or relevant tissue exposure.

Source-supported physiological or viability constraints can exclude parameter/schedule regions only within their measurement context. Absence of one toxicity marker does not exclude other toxicities. Do not automatically use a simulated platelet threshold or published toxicity pattern as a universal safe human schedule limit.

For the primary identified set, distinguish empirical support from assumptions. Perform assumption deletion/relaxation and structural sensitivity. Where source data do not bound a key rate, say 'unbounded/unknown' or seek an analytic limit; do not manufacture a finite biological range simply to make sampling convenient. Log-scale computational cutoffs are approximation controls, not evidence.

Posterior schedule probabilities may be reported only conditionally on stated likelihoods/priors/model weights and with prior sensitivity; they are not probabilities that the treatment works clinically. No success probability for future laboratory work is assigned without evidence. E_LAST availability scenarios are logical branches, not forecast probabilities.
