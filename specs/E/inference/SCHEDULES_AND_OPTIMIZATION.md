# Scheduling without a predetermined winner

Freeze the primary epigenetic-first versus concurrent contrast before model outcomes are inspected. Secondary fixed scenarios may include monotherapies, reverse order, pulse/washout and alternative lead-ins only where their exposure definition is defensible. A schedule record contains amounts and units, route/formulation, actual administration events, source versus hypothetical status, global time origin, exposure summaries, observation horizon and comparison class.

Actual source regimens remain distinct from hypothetical research scenarios. A source-defined human regimen may inform exposure reconstruction; simulated comparisons do not recommend clinical treatment. No numerical drug dose, lead-in or sample size is frozen in this revision.

## Fairness audit

Same agents and total amount per agent, same number of administrations and same burden horizon for a sequence contrast. Record the lead-in as part of treatment time; no invisible pretreatment. Audit AUC to H, Cmax, antibody occupancy duration and residual tails. If equal dose causes unequal exposure in nonlinear PK, explicitly separate 'dose-matched sequence' from 'exposure-standardised sensitivity'; do not mislabel them. A longer/higher-dose arm is a treatment-policy comparison, not pure timing.

## Fixed comparisons before optimisation

Compute the primary admissible contrast set and all prespecified fixed comparisons first. Optimisation is optional secondary work. Restrict feasible schedules using documented pharmacology and source-backed safety/viability constraints, not an invented human therapeutic window. Where no safety constraint is supported, optimisation cannot claim clinically acceptable schedules.

Use distinct optimisation and evaluation parameter samples, alternative structures, joint distributions and numerical tolerances. Report boundary optima, sensitivity and failure rates. Robustness in held-out simulations is not independent biological validation. Formal synergy requires a compatible null model and actual corresponding observations; a favourable simulated combination alone is not synergy.

Allow no stable ranking. Record the assumptions under which ranking reverses and the measurement that would discriminate them. C-IDENTIFIABILITY can close with a demonstrated unresolved contrast; a failed numerical search or arbitrary prior sweep alone cannot.
