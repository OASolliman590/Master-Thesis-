| Gap | Public resolution | If unresolved | Owner / gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recoverable S001 numerical inputs | Audit public article, supplement, FDA review and original PK studies | Independent reimplementation; do not claim exact reproduction | Pharmacometric curator / E1–E2 |

| Population covariance/prevalence | Public joint targets or defensible alternatives | Parameter ensemble, not calibrated patient population | Statistician / E6 |
| Public numeric biology absent | Bounded expansion and lead reassessment | C-INCOMPLETE; do not substitute local experiments automatically | Scientific lead / E0 |

No gap is closed with a biological value from a planned assay. Numerical parameters are tagged measured, reported, digitised, fitted, prior, assumption or unresolved. Dimensionless mathematical fixtures can test an equation but never supply an empirical range.

`PARAMETER_COVERAGE_V2.tsv` assigns public routes and unresolved actions. Local assay/inventory gaps are in E_LAST and do not block unrelated core work.
