# Optional longitudinal cellular and multi-omics corroboration

Use the shared MULTIOMICS_PAIRING_CONTRACT and A/CELLULAR_AND_MULTIOMICS_CONTEXT; A1 adds time/episode requirements rather than a second modality store. A modality bridge must identify patient, specimen, collection event and index episode. Report total modality observations, linked patients, same-timepoint pairs, same-specimen pairs, endpoint-eligible pairs and serial pairs separately. Bulk RNA and methylation co-availability in one study does not establish pairing.

For longitudinal RNA/protein/chromatin comparisons, retain each assay's scale and time window. Concordant direction is orthogonal support, not proof that one modality causes another. Single-cell/spatial cells and spots are nested within donor/specimen; donor remains the clinical unit. Changes in inferred cell fractions may explain a bulk signal but do not establish tumour-intrinsic regulation. Tumour/blood comparisons are explicitly cross-compartment.

Do not select features on the small paired subset and then call evaluation on the same subset independent. All reserved A-P2 families remain protected across modalities. Missing modality data block only the relevant corroboration; no imputation of unobserved assays, fabricated patients or RNA-N inflation. A1's central interaction remains estimable only through its RNA/source-specific release, not by borrowing unpaired modality endpoints.

Outputs contain modality role, pairing class, exact time/episode, patient/specimen/measurement N, effect scale, source support, contradiction and selection limitations. Valid observations may contextualize A/B/C or inform later D/E model hypotheses; they do not directly supply causal transition rates, drug-specific efficacy or PK/PD parameters.
