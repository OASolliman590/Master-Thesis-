# A1 validation and four-figure architecture

## Mandatory checks

Validate episode-specific outcome assignment, exact/source-supported timing, patient-aware pairing, row-order invariance, multiple follow-ups, lesion/platform policy, missing-biopsy audit and protected A-P2 family exclusion. Statistical tests must include known synthetic interaction sign, a rank-deficient patient-plus-response design, a valid full-rank contrast, shared baseline dependence and one-response-group nonestimability. 'Significant in one group only' must never pass as an interaction result.

## Four-figure plan

Every panel includes source/release, unique patient N, specimen/assay N, experimental unit, method, uncertainty and claim ceiling. All N are UNKNOWN until receipts exist. A missing gate yields an explicit unavailable panel, not an invented data plot.

| Panel | Question / source | Experimental unit / counts | Analysis and uncertainty | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1A | What longitudinal sources exist? Study/episode registry | Independent study families; patient N separately UNKNOWN | Source flow, regimen/window coverage | Scope and gaps, not catalogue-as-cohort count |
| 1B | Which patients are genuinely paired? Pair/missingness receipts | Patients; specimens/assays separately | Timing diagram, exact days/categories, same-lesion and selection flow | Verified pair structure and selected population |
| 2A | What changes within groups? Released paired effects | Paired patients | DELTA_R/DELTA_NR intervals | Group-specific observed change, not evidence of an interaction by p-value contrast |
| 2B | Do changes differ by response? Direct interaction output | Patients within study | Response-time estimates/intervals and compatible synthesis | Response-associated differential change |
| 2C | Which programme directions replicate? Full programme tables | Independent families/cancers | Support/direction/influence and uncertainty | Qualified replication or contradiction |
| 3A | What context is identifiable? Moderator design audit | Independent studies plus paired patients | Cancer/regimen/class/window cross-tab, rank/confounding map | Which comparisons can and cannot be separated |
| 3B | How do estimable effects vary? Released moderator outputs | Independent families | Prespecified interaction/meta-regression intervals | Context-associated differences, not drug efficacy ranking |
| 3C | How sensitive are estimates? Lesion/timing/selection sensitivity | Same or explicitly changed patient populations | Matched sensitivity and influence with intervals | Robustness limits; no assumption that missing biopsies are random |
| 4A | Is there orthogonal cellular evidence? Modality/context release | Patients; nested cells/spots separately | Source-qualified localization/protein/chromatin support | Mechanistic context, not causal proof |
| 4B | What can comparative/mechanistic consumers use? NMA audit and handoff | Studies/programmes | Feasibility map and qualified temporal evidence graph | Observational input to A/B/C/D/E with unsupported arrows absent |

## Publication gate

Before viewing positive results, specify the distinct question, independent family support, patient pairing, compatible windows/effects, achievable precision, novelty/overlap and a coherent four-figure contribution. One study cannot be described as cross-study replication. A statistically null but precise context analysis can be publishable; significance is not the promotion rule. Baseline patients shared with A are disclosed, and baseline results are referenced rather than duplicated as independent discoveries. If standalone evidence is insufficient, retain this full workstream in the thesis or as an explicitly bounded module without devaluing valid analyses.
