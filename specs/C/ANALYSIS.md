# Paper C — Proposed analysis contract

All numerical design choices here are proposals, not observed effects or user-approved endpoints. Freeze them before scoring. The primary is a measured assay outcome; statistical class superiority and clinical sensitisation are not identified by the current design.

## Disease query and development boundary

Primary input is one signed B discovery-derived gene list, with a frozen hash and per-gene provenance. Define disease-up and disease-down relative to the same stated disease axis. Neither C drug rankings, held-out B patient outcomes nor immune-validation outcomes may choose query genes, directions, thresholds or size. The B handoff must state its exact expression/methylation model, covariates, genome/gene annotation, effect direction and multiplicity rule. Those choices remain blocked until B's discovery contract is settled; this kit does not silently substitute the eight-gene B expression endpoint for a bidirectional disease query.

A query lacking one direction is not eligible for the proposed bidirectional primary. Report that scientific/data outcome; obtain a reviewed one-sided endpoint amendment before scoring rather than manufacturing genes to fill the other direction. Historical 150-up/150-down and 8-of-18 canonical-gene gates are not adopted. Size sensitivity uses a finite list of prespecified sizes justified after outcome-blind coverage, not selection by reversal strength. Background genes must be those actually supplied to the scorer, with landmark/best-inferred/other-inferred/absent membership retained.

Within-PRAD and PRAD-versus-LUAD are separate disease contrasts. Tissue differences cannot validate an immune-state effect. An expression-only query from the same discovery patients and identical selection budget is a prespecified comparator to the methylation-supported query; its derivation must be frozen before C scoring. Neither is a treatment-response label.

## One primary endpoint

For each protocol compound c, the proposed primary endpoint is **condition-specific reversal R_c**, calculated at PC3, 24 h, nominal 1.11111 micromolar, using all unique QC-passing Level5 signatures for that identity and condition. This is the lowest common measured QC-passing exposure in the completed metadata audit. Choice of this anchor uses coverage only; clinical feasibility is not established. It is a material choice for review.

For a perturbation vector z over an ordered fixed gene universe G, rank descending by z. For query set S, the weighted running sum at position i is:

`RS_S(i) = sum_{g in S, rank(g)<=i} |z_g| / sum_{g in S}|z_g| - count_{g not in S, rank(g)<=i}/(|G|-|S|)`.

ES(S,z) is the signed extreme of this curve. Exact equal absolute positive/negative extremes, tied z ranks and zero total hit weights must follow the pinned reference implementation's verified convention; until numerical fixtures resolve these details, the scorer is not ready. Empty S, complete-universe S, nonfinite required values and overlapping UP/DOWN sets reject that query, not return a successful zero.

`WTCS(z) = (ES(UP,z) - ES(DOWN,z))/2` when `sign(ES(UP,z)) != sign(ES(DOWN,z))`, otherwise 0, matching the pinned `getCombinedES` call path with `rescale=false`. Here sign(0)=0: exactly one zero ES retains the half-difference; two zeros give zero. This explicit boundary is more precise than saying strictly opposite nonzero signs. `R_c = -mean_{s in S_c} WTCS(z_s)`, after removal of duplicated instance sets and a documented policy for partial instance overlap. Higher R means stronger reversal, range [-1,1]. Positive WTCS means disease-concordant direction. The exact-oracle fixtures must cover both signed extremes, same-direction sets, zero boundaries and ties.

The experimental observation is a collapsed signature conditional on its experiment/project, not an independent patient or every constituent cell/gene. Proposed equal signature weighting describes this frozen release. It does not turn two decitabine signatures and one tazemetostat signature into replicated randomised drug arms. If partial overlaps cannot be resolved without source-level reaggregation, that condition remains blocked; no pseudoreplicate bootstrap is allowed.

There is **one outcome with three compound-specific estimates**, not three different primary endpoints. Report all three and their component signatures. The common-exposure figure must show project identity: tazemetostat's MOAR005 project does not overlap the other compounds' projects at the anchor. Between-drug differences cannot be assigned a treatment-superiority p-value from these data.

## Null comparisons, uncertainty, multiplicity and precision

The primary finite-release estimate is descriptive. There is no justified biological population CI at an anchor with one experiment for a compound. Report component scores and coverage; do not bootstrap genes or collapsed signatures as independent biological units. Statistical precision for a biological drug effect requires new independent experiments or verified independent biological replicates with appropriate controls.

A separately labelled **query-specificity analysis** may compare observed R to random queries from the same assayed universe, preserving UP/DOWN sizes and measured/inferred strata. Prespecify a fixed RNG seed, 10,000 draws as a resolution choice (minimum attainable Monte Carlo p about 1/10,001), and the one-sided statistic `p=(1+sum(R_null>=R_observed))/(1+K)`. Adjust the three planned-compound query-null tests by Benjamini–Hochberg as one declared family. This is not a p-value for a biological drug effect: genes are not generally exchangeable, so matched-query null validity must be assessed and reported. It does not preserve all gene correlation or functional-selection structure and cannot certify specificity on its own. If a justified null cannot be defined, retain the descriptive endpoint without an inferential claim.

Thresholds, ranking cutoffs and class hypotheses must not be chosen after viewing scores. No required positive effect, canonical recovery threshold or favorable class ordering gates technical success. Report missing uncertainty honestly; do not promise publishability from an under-replicated assay contrast.

## Prespecified secondary and exploratory arms

1. **Broader LINCS screen:** curate epigenetic compound identities/mechanisms before scoring; retain every eligible prostate context and actual dose/time. Report context-specific ranks and coverage, not an average dominated by drugs with many signatures. PC3 anchor comparisons at 3.33333 and10 micromolar and all-HIQ restriction are sensitivity analyses; higher dose/HIQ is not chosen because it produces stronger biology.
2. **Class question:** exposure-/context-specific compound summaries are independent units only after identity/instance curation. Estimate a class contrast only if multiple distinct compounds and project/context overlap identify it. Test rank and collinearity of the proposed design before fitting; random effects cannot repair perfect class/project confounding. Otherwise publish descriptive class coverage and restrict claims. The model formula and minimum precision target require a design amendment once the actual curated panel is known.
3. **Methylation contribution:** compare frozen methylation-supported and expression-only queries under identical perturbation/scoring conditions. Show rank stability and discordant candidate interpretations. Quantify top-k/rank choices only after predeclaring k; prioritize independent measured checks of disputed interpretations rather than calling changed ranks improved accuracy.
4. **Measured genes:** primary mixed L1000 gene-space evidence is explicitly tiered. Of the ten sentinels CXCL9/10,HLA-A/B/C,B2M,TAP1/2,PSMB8/9, only PSMB8 is landmark in the audited file. Landmark-only results cannot validate all ten. Require independent measured expression/protein where available; absent readouts remain absent.
5. **Cell and sensitivity context:** baseline CCLE expression/methylation and DepMap dependency annotate model relevance; PRISM/GDSC measure growth sensitivity. Do not regress ten contexts as if thousands of gene rows create biological N. Preserve assay scale, exposure and source overlap. Target essentiality alone does not establish direct chromatin regulation of an immune gene.
6. **Immune-context triangulation:** use Perturb-CITE-seq genetic effects in its own human melanoma/IFNG/co-culture setting; TISMO in each original mouse/animal experiment; LJP4 only with verified payload. Select controls and units before evaluating outcomes. Do not meta-analyse unlike species, assays and endpoints into one causal evidence score.
7. **PK:** compare nominal assay concentrations to documented human exposure as a plausibility annotation, not a hard proof of achievable tumour exposure. Extract formulation, regimen, steady state, route, total/unbound concentration, binding method, active metabolite, sampling time and units from original human PK/regulatory sources. If fu_plasma is known, Cu_plasma=fu_plasma*Ctotal is a calculation with assumptions; nominal in-vitro dose cannot be equated with free medium/intracellular concentration when binding is unknown. DNMTi time/divisions and EZH2 delayed chromatin responses limit 24 h interpretations. Cmax alone does not establish exposure-duration equivalence.

Each additional outcome family gets a named estimand, unit and multiplicity policy before inferential execution. Unresolved arms remain specified conditional modules, not opportunistic analyses or completed validation.
