The broad Paper C concept, historical three-compound anchor, LINCS metadata coverage, candidate source identities and scientific concerns were reviewed before this amendment. No perturbation-expression outcome was inspected to choose the C3 framework or paired primary design. Earlier design exposure must remain disclosed in any preregistration or manuscript.

## Primary precommitments before outcome access

Freeze and hash:

1. A programme version and desired direction;
2. B discovery patients and disease contrast;
3. expression eligibility/ranking rule;
4. methylation-support gate;
5. programme/direction quotas;
6. `Q_E` and `Q_M` rows and query hashes;
7. independent source-admission rules;
8. source-specific primary contrasts and covariates;
9. coverage thresholds;
10. sample-level scorer version;
11. aggregation and multiplicity plan;
12. missingness and exclusion rules.

## Amendment policy

An amendment after outcome access must state:

- date and decision owner;
- data/results already seen;
- exact old and new rule;
- scientific reason;
- affected analyses;
- whether the result becomes exploratory;
- preserved original output.

No result-driven amendment may be backdated or presented as prespecified.

## Candidate freeze

C2 may execute only after query and scorer release. Candidate interpretations are frozen before independent validation outcomes are opened. Independent-source availability may be audited before discovery, but outcome direction may not determine which contrast is eligible.

## Negative findings

No positive reversal threshold, canonical-drug recovery or favourable methylation ordering defines technical success. Null, opposite-direction and context-specific results remain in primary tables and figures.
