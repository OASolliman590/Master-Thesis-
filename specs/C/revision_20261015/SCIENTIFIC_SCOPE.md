# Scientific scope and claim hierarchy

## Paper-level role

Paper C is the pharmacological bridge between patient evidence and prospective experiments. It is not a generic repurposing pipeline and not a computational demonstration of ICI sensitisation.

### Primary scientific question

Does patient-supported methylation evidence enrich a clinically anchored prostate immune-barrier query for genes that respond in the desired direction under independent measured epigenetic perturbation, compared with an equally sized expression-only query derived from the same discovery patients?

### Translational question

Which compounds, models and exposure windows remain defensible for prospective testing after discovery connectivity, independent measured response, model relevance, target/mechanism evidence, viability/stress and human pharmacology are kept separate and jointly interpreted?

## Claim ladder

1. **Measured molecular perturbation:** a treatment changes a signed molecular programme in a specified model and exposure.
2. **Mechanistic consistency:** target engagement, genetic perturbation or chromatin/protein evidence is compatible with the chemical effect.
3. **Immune-functional effect:** an experiment shows altered immune recognition, killing, cytokine response or another defined immune function.
4. **Combination interaction:** a factorial experiment estimates an interaction between epigenetic priming and ICI.
5. **Clinical benefit:** requires clinical comparative evidence and is outside Paper C's computational estimand.

Evidence at a lower level cannot be relabelled as a higher-level claim.

## Success does not require

- a positive average perturbation effect;
- recovery of a familiar drug;
- a new compound;
- class superiority;
- agreement between every source;
- a favourable methylation result.

A null, negative or context-dependent result is scientifically valid when the endpoint, units and missingness are preserved.

## Standalone-publication gate

A standalone manuscript is justified only if the final evidence contains more than a familiar ranked list. At minimum it should contain a frozen A/B handoff, a fair `Q_M` versus `Q_E` comparison, independent measured perturbation evidence, context/stress analysis, transparent contradictions and a prospective experimental handoff. If independent evidence cannot test what methylation adds, C should be combined with B or retained as a thesis-only pharmacological module.
