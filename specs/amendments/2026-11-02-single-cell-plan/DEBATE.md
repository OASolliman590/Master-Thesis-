## Astra (gpt-6-astra, high)

**C1 — CONCEDE (Astra’s 30–40 hours).** With only descriptive SC-1 retained, 20 researcher-hours is a reasonable stopping rule, not a promise that both figures will be feasible. Qualification failures should reduce scope rather than extend the budget.  
**FROZEN DEFAULT:** ≤20 hours total: Lin inspection ≤3, Paper A SC-1 ≤6, Paper B qualification and SC-1 ≤8, shared checking/reporting ≤3.

**C2 — DEFEND (Astra’s single-study scope).** Separate facets prevent numerical pooling but still multiply qualification, annotation and interpretation work. One development-relevant cancer and one qualified source study are sufficient for the proposed localisation claim.  
**FROZEN DEFAULT:** One Lin source study, one cancer, baseline ICI-only samples, selected using relevance and metadata feasibility before examining expression concordance; one specimen per donor under a frozen selection rule.

**C3 — DEFEND (Astra’s “not assessed”).** A separate Jerby-Arnon facet would avoid direct scale mixing but would not supply the missing malignant compartment for the selected cancer and source. The extra acquisition and qualification are unnecessary for this supporting analysis.  
**FROZEN DEFAULT:** If the selected source lacks supported malignant-cell annotations, report malignant expression as “not assessed”; no Jerby-Arnon supplement.

**C4 — CONCEDE (Astra’s principles without numbers).** Concrete operating rules are preferable, provided these cutoffs are labelled pragmatic reporting thresholds rather than biological validation or power guarantees. The 5,000-UMI rule applies only to verified UMI counts; read counts cannot silently substitute for UMIs. Missing features remain distinct from measured zeros, and composite scoring must retain its frozen definition.  
**FROZEN DEFAULT:** Use verified UMI data; sum counts per study × donor × selected baseline specimen × broad compartment; mark profiles NA below 15 cells or 5,000 UMIs; display expression only with ≥5 eligible donors; use log₂(CPM+1), equal-donor medians and donor-level percentages of cells with counts >0, reporting donor support/variation and ambient-RNA caveats; unavailable required genes make the corresponding composite unavailable.

**C5 — DEFEND (Astra’s Paper B priority).** There is no substantive difference if Argon’s “must-do” means a mandatory bounded attempt rather than a mandatory result. Both positions prioritise prostate context and allow omission when a suitable reference is unavailable.  
**FROZEN DEFAULT:** Reserve Paper B’s ≤8-hour attempt first; drop Paper A SC-1 first when time runs short, and drop Paper B SC-1 too if qualification or completion exceeds its cap.

**ONE NEW RISK:** Neither review explicitly fixes the CPM denominator. Computing library totals after subsetting to panel genes would make expression depend on the selected panel; freeze the denominator as the full retained gene-count library before extracting targets.

### ONE NEW RISK

- **Droplet zero-inflation (false negatives on M6 chromatin regulators) and within-panel abundance dominance:** Both reviews guarded against *false-positive* cross-compartment bleed from ambient RNA (`HLA-I`, `B2M`, `CXCL9`), but neither addressed *false-negative* dropout on low-copy transcripts in 3' droplet scRNA-seq or scale dominance inside composite panels. Chromatin modifiers and transcription factors in Paper B's M6 module (`DNMT1`, `DNMT3A/B`, `EZH2`, `NSD2`, `HDAC*`) and rate-limiting cytokines (`IFNG`) are typically expressed at $5\text{–}30\text{ TPM}$ in bulk RNA-seq, which yields an expected $0.025\text{–}0.15\text{ UMIs}$ in a minimum $5,000\text{-UMI}$ pseudobulk library ($>85\%$ probability of observing zero counts), while averaging unstandardised $\log_2(\text{CPM}+1)$ across a panel lets housekeeping-level transcripts (`B2M`, `HLA-A/B/C`) mask low-copy components (`NLRC5`, `TAP1`). **Guardrail:** Never use scRNA-seq as a negative screen to rule out M6 regulators—genes with donor-median expression $< 50\text{ CPM}$ across *all* sampled compartments must be tagged `below_scRNA_detection_limit` rather than `absent` or `immune_stromal_restricted` in `biological_context.tsv`, and SC-1 figures must pair panel summaries with individual-gene dotplots (without z-scoring across compartments).
