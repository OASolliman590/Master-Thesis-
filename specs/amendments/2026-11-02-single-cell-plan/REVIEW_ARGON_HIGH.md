### VERDICT
**Rewrite (and cut ~75% of the proposed scope):** Cut SC-3, cut A.1 single-cell, and permanently cut SC-2; keep at most a strictly time-boxed (≤15–20 hours total) broad-compartment expression-localisation lookup (SC-1) in Paper B and optionally Paper A, or cut Paper A single-cell entirely if inspecting the Lin 2026 object reveals missing raw counts or provenance gaps.

### FINDINGS

1. **HIGH — Overall Scope ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md)): Single-cell scope expands back beyond the 600-hour MSc budget and conflicts with the earlier review's cap.**
   - *Problem:* While claiming that [REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md) and [DRAFT.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/DRAFT.md) still apply (which restricted work to 30–40 hours covering one prostate reference plus at most one Paper A cancer), the new plan expands to a 10-cancer, 77-subtype atlas, up to five extra scRNA cohorts (`Jerby-Arnon`, `Sade-Feldman`, `Yost`, `Bi`, `Fuchs`), SC-3 responder testing, and A.1 longitudinal transitions while core bulk pipelines in Papers A and B remain unfinished. Paper A's primary and secondary claims (A-P2 bulk predictor, 20-cohort panel meta-analysis, paired endpoint sensitivity) do not need single-cell data at all.
   - *Fix/default:* Hard-cap all single-cell work across the thesis at **20 hours total**. Cut SC-3, A.1 single-cell, SC-2, and all secondary scRNA cohort downloads (`Sade-Feldman`, `Yost`, `Bi`, `Fuchs`, and the 4 Zenodo Visium slides). If Paper A's bulk pipeline is behind schedule or the Lin object fails any inspection gate below, **cut single-cell from Paper A completely** and keep only the bulk 59-gene comparator.

2. **HIGH — Sources & Order of Work ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md), [SINGLE_CELL.tsv](docs/cohorts/SINGLE_CELL.tsv)): Reusing the Lin 2026 integrated Seurat object carries severe provenance, integration, and cohort-rule risks.**
   - *Problem:* An uninspected 4.0 GB integrated Seurat object may store only batch-corrected residuals or 2,000–3,000 highly variable genes rather than full-transcriptome raw integer counts, likely lacks malignant cells ("77 immune and stromal subtypes"), and almost certainly pools CD45+-sorted with unsorted biopsies, multiple chemistries, patients overlapping our bulk [REGISTRY.tsv](docs/cohorts/REGISTRY.tsv), and combination chemo+ICI trials (e.g., `GSE207422` or `Zhang 2021`) that the signed [AMENDMENT.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-29-integrated-plan/AMENDMENT.md) explicitly excludes. It also fails to check whether any atlas sample belongs to the locked `imvigor210` study family, violating [VALIDATION_AND_SPLITS.md](specs/A/VALIDATION_AND_SPLITS.md) and [REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md).
   - *Fix/default:* Make step 1 a strict pass/fail inspection script on the Seurat object's metadata and assay layers before any analysis: (a) verify unnormalised integer `counts` exist across all locked panel and M5/M6 genes, (b) extract exact originating study accessions, (c) drop all chemo+ICI / non-ICI combination samples, (d) verify `patient_id`, `specimen_id`, `timepoint`, and sorting (`CD45+` vs unsorted) columns, (e) check whether malignant/epithelial cells are present, (f) cross-check all source studies against [REGISTRY.tsv](docs/cohorts/REGISTRY.tsv) and `imvigor210` for patient/family overlap, and (g) hash and pin the file per [AMENDMENT.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-31-simple-mode/AMENDMENT.md). If (a), (b), or (d) is missing, reject the atlas.

3. **HIGH — Paper A SC-1 ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md)): Localising panels across Lin's 77 subtypes by 10 cancers is circular, sparse, and incompatible with patching Smart-seq2 melanoma malignant cells.**
   - *Problem:* Lin's 77 immune/stromal subtypes were defined using marker genes that overlap our 11 locked immune panels (`IFNG`, `CXCL9`, `GZMB`, `PDCD1`, `HLA-*`), making enrichment across those 77 clusters tautological while splitting 241 patients across `10 cancers × 77 subtypes` leaves most cells below the 5-donor floor ([REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md), [#L129-L134](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md)). Furthermore, splicing Smart-seq2 melanoma malignant cells from `Jerby-Arnon 2018` onto a 10x droplet atlas mixes incompatible measurement scales and treats neural-crest melanoma cells as a proxy for malignant epithelial cells across carcinomas.
   - *Fix/default:* Collapse the 77 subtypes into **5–6 broad canonical compartments** (T/NK, B/plasma, myeloid, fibroblast/stromal, endothelial, plus malignant/epithelial *only* inside unsorted whole-tumour studies). Aggregate to patient-level profiles first (`study × patient × broad_compartment`, requiring $\ge 15$ cells and $\ge 5$ donors per compartment), keep CD45+-sorted and Smart-seq2 cohorts in separate facet panels on their own within-study scales, and restrict the claim strictly to **broad-lineage expression presence within sampled compartments**.

4. **HIGH — Paper A SC-3 ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md), [#L37-L37](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md)): Cut SC-3; it is underpowered, redundant with Lin 2026 and bulk Paper A, and a frozen feasibility table does not prevent outcome peeking.**
   - *Problem:* Stratifying baseline monotherapy ICI patients by `cancer × endpoint × cell_type` leaves tiny sample sizes that cannot survive BH FDR correction across 11 panels + individual M5/M6 genes × cell types, duplicates Lin 2026's own responder analysis on the same patients, and ignores the signed rule against pooling different response definitions (ORR vs clinical benefit, [AMENDMENT.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-29-integrated-plan/AMENDMENT.md)). Moreover, freezing a `(patients per cancer, cell type, and response)` table before scoring **does not protect against outcome peeking**: (a) letting the user decide whether and where SC-3 runs *after* inspecting responder/non-responder counts per cell type allows post-hoc selection of responder-enriched cell states (since cell-type capture in the atlas correlates with outcome), (b) Lin 2026 and Sade-Feldman 2018 outcomes on these cohorts are already published, and (c) pseudobulk normalisation, minimum cell counts, and multiplicity families remain unfixed.
   - *Fix/default:* **Cut SC-3 completely.** Let the ~1,257 bulk patients in [REGISTRY.tsv](docs/cohorts/REGISTRY.tsv) carry all responder vs non-responder testing.

5. **HIGH — Paper A.1 ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md), [#L38-L38](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md)): Cut single-cell A.1; re-running pre/on transitions in the Lin atlas is derivative of Lin 2026, and Fuchs 2026 violates the combination-outcome exclusion.**
   - *Problem:* Lin et al. (*Cancer Cell* 2026) specifically built this longitudinal atlas to show that on-treatment TIME/cell-state transitions occur in ~40% of tumours and predict ICI response ([NOVELTY_CHECK_20261029.md](docs/research/NOVELTY_CHECK_20261029.md)); re-analysing their pre/on pairs to show the same thing will be judged derivative by reviewers, and comparing unpaired bulk and scRNA cohorts cannot prove bulk deltas reflect cell-state shifts rather than cell influx. In addition, `Fuchs 2026` measures clinical response after **immunochemotherapy/chemoradiotherapy** ([NOVELTY_CHECK_20261029.md](docs/research/NOVELTY_CHECK_20261029.md)), so using it for A.1's response-stratified change contrasts ([INTERFACES_AND_GATES.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-16-immune-programmes/INTERFACES_AND_GATES.md)) conflicts with the signed combination-therapy exclusion ([AMENDMENT.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-29-integrated-plan/AMENDMENT.md)).
   - *Fix/default:* **Cut single-cell A.1.** Keep A.1 strictly on paired bulk cohorts (and within-study randomised arms per [AMENDMENT.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-29-integrated-plan/AMENDMENT.md)), and cite Lin 2026 and Yost 2019 in the Discussion as published single-cell corroboration.

6. **MEDIUM — Paper B SC-1 & SC-2 ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md)): Clarify the narrow negative-filter role of prostate scRNA, time-box the search, and permanently cut SC-2.**
   - *Problem:* Because scRNA lacks methylation, a prostate scRNA reference cannot support positive claims of epigenetic silencing; its only value for Paper B is flagging whether a methylation-associated gene in bulk TCGA-PRAD is actually expressed in prostate malignant/luminal epithelial cells (plausible tumour-intrinsic candidate, e.g., HLA-I/APM or M6 regulators) versus restricted to infiltrating leukocytes (where bulk methylation–RNA correlation is an infiltrate/purity artefact). Keeping SC-2 "reconsidered if a qualified prostate reference exists" invites RNA-on-RNA circularity ([REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md)) and duplicates Paper B's existing `Qpure` and `MethylCIBERSORT` sensitivity tracks ([IMMUNE_BARRIER_FRAMEWORK.md](specs/B/IMMUNE_BARRIER_FRAMEWORK.md)).
   - *Fix/default:* **Permanently cut SC-2.** Time-box Paper B SC-1 to **≤8 hours** using one pre-annotated, unsorted, donor-resolved primary prostate adenocarcinoma matrix from CELLxGENE or GEO to classify Paper B genes in `biological_context.tsv` as `epithelial_expressed` vs `immune_stromal_restricted` across 5–6 broad compartments; if no turnkey matrix passes licence and donor checks within 8 hours, drop Paper B scRNA and use TCGA/CPC purity adjustments plus literature/HPA annotations.

7. **MEDIUM — Paper A Comparator ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md)): Incomplete overlap check on Lin's discovery atlas, missing G01/G03 algorithm check, and imprecise IMvigor210 label handling.**
   - *Problem:* Checking only Lin's 19 *bulk validation* cohorts misses whether any of Lin's *241 scRNA discovery patients* overlap our bulk cohorts in [REGISTRY.tsv](docs/cohorts/REGISTRY.tsv) (which creates training leakage, not merely `PREVIOUSLY_INSPECTED` validation overlap), and [PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md) admits the 59-gene signature without verifying that exact gene weights, signs, and scoring rules are published ([INTERFACES_AND_GATES.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-16-immune-programmes/INTERFACES_AND_GATES.md) Gates G01/G03). It also omits the `Subramanian 2023` comparator identified in [NOVELTY_CHECK_20261029.md](docs/research/NOVELTY_CHECK_20261029.md).
   - *Fix/default:* Move comparator admission to the Paper A **bulk** spec (alongside `Subramanian 2023`), require published weights/formula before admission, check both Lin's scRNA discovery studies and its 19 bulk validation cohorts against [REGISTRY.tsv](docs/cohorts/REGISTRY.tsv), and record `PREVIOUSLY_INSPECTED_EXTERNAL` strictly as a **comparator-specific metadata tag** on `imvigor210` ([VALIDATION_AND_SPLITS.md](specs/A/VALIDATION_AND_SPLITS.md)) while keeping `imvigor210` `UNTOUCHED_FINAL_TEST` for our learner and locked in code until final evaluation.

8. **MEDIUM — Rules Carried Over ([PLAN.md](specs/amendments/2026-11-02-single-cell-plan/PLAN.md)): Missing quantitative cutoffs for under-sampled cell types, pseudobulk normalisation, and ambient RNA.**
   - *Problem:* Saying "scores come from raw counts within each source study" and "under-sampled cell types give missing scores, not zero" leaves the actual pseudobulk transformation, minimum cells per `patient × compartment`, minimum donors per compartment, and ambient-RNA false-positive guard (critical when high-abundance `HLA-I`, `B2M`, or `CXCL9` transcripts bleed into droplets of other lineages) undefined ([REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md), [#L129-L134](specs/amendments/2026-10-30-single-cell-module/REVIEW_ASTRA_HIGH.md)).
   - *Fix/default:* Freeze concrete defaults in the spec before running SC-1: sum raw integer counts per `study × patient × baseline_specimen × broad_compartment`, mark any `patient × compartment` with $<15$ cells or $<5,000$ total UMIs as `NA` (missing), require $\ge 5$ eligible donors to display a compartment in a study, normalise pseudobulk libraries as $\log_2(\text{CPM}+1)$, and report both donor-median expression and donor-level % expressing cells alongside an explicit ambient-RNA/dissociation caveat.

### MINIMAL PLAN

- **Global budget & cuts:** Cap all single-cell work at **≤15–20 hours total**. Cut **SC-3**, **A.1 single-cell**, **SC-2**, the 4 Zenodo Visium samples, and standalone downloads of `Sade-Feldman`, `Yost`, `Bi`, and `Fuchs 2026`.
- **Gate 0 (Lin 2026 Seurat metadata inspection, ≤3 hours):** Run a read-only script on the downloaded Zenodo object to verify (1) full-transcriptome raw integer `counts`, (2) source-study IDs, (3) treatment regimens (excluding chemo+ICI combinations), (4) donor, baseline timepoint, and `CD45+` vs unsorted flags, (5) whether malignant cells exist, and (6) zero overlap with `imvigor210`'s family. If raw counts or donor/study links are missing, **drop single-cell from Paper A completely**.
- **Paper A SC-1 (optional, ≤6 hours if Gate 0 passes):** Using baseline ICI-only samples from the inspected Lin object (plus `Jerby-Arnon 2018` plotted as a separate Smart-seq2 unsorted melanoma facet if the atlas lacks malignant cells), collapse cell labels to **5–6 broad canonical compartments** (T/NK, B/plasma, myeloid, fibroblast/stromal, endothelial, and malignant/epithelial where unsorted). Sum raw counts per `study × patient × broad_compartment` ($\ge 15$ cells, $\ge 5$ donors), compute $\log_2(\text{CPM}+1)$ scores for the 11 locked panels and frozen top A-DE genes, and output **one broad-compartment localisation figure** and **one provenance/coverage table**.
- **Paper A Bulk Comparator (moved out of single-cell plan):** Add Lin's 59-gene signature and `Subramanian 2023` to the bulk comparator registry only if their exact published weights/algorithms pass Gate G01/G03; audit both Lin's scRNA discovery studies and its 19 bulk validation cohorts against [REGISTRY.tsv](docs/cohorts/REGISTRY.tsv) and tag `imvigor210` with comparator-specific `PREVIOUSLY_INSPECTED_EXTERNAL` metadata without touching `imvigor210` data.
- **Paper B SC-1 (≤8 hours):** Take **one** pre-annotated, unsorted, donor-resolved primary prostate adenocarcinoma scRNA matrix (from CELLxGENE/GEO), aggregate per `patient × broad_compartment` (luminal/malignant epithelial, basal epithelial, T/NK, myeloid, B/plasma, stromal/endothelial; $\ge 15$ cells, $\ge 5$ donors), and produce **one dotplot/heatmap and one lookup column** in `biological_context.tsv` tagging Paper B's panels and M6 regulators as `epithelial_expressed` vs `immune_stromal_restricted`. If no clean dataset is ready within 8 hours, stop and rely on `Qpure` / `MethylCIBERSORT` and literature annotations.
