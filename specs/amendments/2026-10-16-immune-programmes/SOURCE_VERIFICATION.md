## Inventor-disclosure recovery, not score release

[US20180327848A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20180327848A1/en), Tables3A/3B Set2.4, supplies 18 nonzero coefficients matching the recovered GEP membership, including negative CD276. Table1C lists 11 normalization genes. The family identifier PCT/US2015/064445 matches Ayers2017's conflict-of-interest disclosure. `ayers_inventor_disclosure.candidate.json` records the exact candidate values and source locators. This is original inventor disclosure, not the JCI supplement. Some patent prose says 10 normalization genes while Table1C lists11; do not silently choose. Assay equivalence, probe/alias mapping, source-byte pinning and transport remain gates. Therefore it is inaccurate to say no coefficients were found, and equally inaccurate to call this an admitted published-score implementation.
