# Result interpretation after a valid gate

A qualified null effect may constrain an amplitude or rule out a large effect in the measured context; a wide interval may not. Lack of qualified checkpoint response does not establish clinical resistance. Lack of drug exposure/target engagement means a negative downstream phenotype cannot identify a biological failure mechanism. Unknown exposure and uncertain carryover remain separate alternative explanations.

If immune impairment or direct toxicity dominates, retain those data and compare the frozen causal attribution with a direct-effect model. If concurrent or reverse sequence performs better, score the original prediction fairly and report the result. If observation disagrees with the model, investigate source/context transfer, assay mapping, non-identifiability and structural inadequacy without fitting away the failed test.

With several independent donors/runs, consider hierarchical context effects only to the degree supported by the data. Unknown covariance is not filled with a universal multivariate normal. Experimental virtual populations describe sampled preparations/donors under the specified laboratory context; they are not prostate-patient populations. Human exposure extrapolations remain secondary and uncertain.

Sparse or inconsistent data can yield a useful descriptive/negative validation report without enabling every module. 'Extension completed with limited capability' names exactly the achieved branch; it does not mean all original experimental aims were achieved. No probability of future positive results or successful data generation is invented.
